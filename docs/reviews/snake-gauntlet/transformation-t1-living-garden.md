## Largest experience defect and intended whole-scene change

The camera currently sees a mostly repetitive playable rectangle with a thin, rectangular supporting edge, disconnected distant geological islands and relatively light scattered vegetation. Snake Pass remains far ahead in authored ecological depth, grounded ruins, vegetation, light and character-scale staging; ASTRO BOT is further ahead in stylized material/scene craft. This is the single largest full-environment transformation defect. A one-percent pixel/texture tweak cannot close it.

The first full-scene candidate introduces an **original cohesive shoreline and ruined garden** surrounding, but never overlapping, the unchanged authoritative gameplay rectangle: a sunlit foreground foreshore, deeper shaded rear cove, fern-heavy west bank, reed-heavy east bank, each with sculpted sloping relief, overhanging stone to the waterline and dense original leaf fans. Broken flanking bastions, retaining walls, ivy-root pockets and consistent moss/stone/foliage vertex-color and weathering response connect the island silhouette. This is new **real 3D geometry** at near/middle/background scales, not a CSS picture. Every scene piece comes from local procedural geometry; no downloaded or licensed game assets.

**Implementation:** `public/snake-stream/living-garden.mjs` creates four merged static batches and one original, deterministic 128×128 shared weathering atlas. Renderer attaches it to the same world's STATIC_LAYER/cached physical WebGL scene and disposes resources on board change/context cleanup. The existing world, serpent, motion, host, AI, immutable snapshot schema, portal/cues/HUD, light/fallback and strict frame gate remain unchanged. No new time-dependent resource uploads or paid services. New garden adds real triangles and full-fragment shading and may aggravate the baseline 16/17 strict performance failure—no performance victory is implied.

## Smallest independently judgeable responsive-design slice

The existing original stage frames the whole board safely in portrait but its oblique view spends too much vertical space on perspective rather than readable playing ground. This R56 candidate adjusts **only portrait overview** camera azimuth/tilt continuously with the true stage aspect, approaching a moderately more overhead perspective on narrow tall displays. Real board, snake, hazards, fruit, scenery, camera target, portal discontinuities and near/far geometry are unchanged. No CSS fake zoom, image asset, shader, extra render pass, draw object, texture, screenshot shortcut, influence on simulation or hidden state. Landscape overview, follow, detail, reduced-motion path, captions and fallback remain intact. The change is exactly source-reversible via one R56 block to original camera Git blob `c40b3dafd5becf38e77c72e7b8d7dba5414f6c12`.

Specification intent is responsive comprehension and original 3D stage framing, not a new gameplay mode. Snake Pass still has far richer animation, face/scale, level art and physically grounded environment; ASTRO BOT lighting/craft also remain well ahead.

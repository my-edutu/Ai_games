Pixel deltas localize the change to the floor. No new render object/shader texture fetch was introduced. This is a small **visual texture treatment**, not a frame-delivery or whole-game quality win.

The current court is still a large planar repetitive grid, face deformation limited and live motion irregular; a line-and-color change does not close that gap.

## Why this small slice

The prior real focused browser metrics at [41178418985](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41178418985) showed 42 draw calls and 39 world draw objects with a populated 3D scene. Six identical-material, fixed-pose edge-foam toruses and two identical-material, fixed-pose water-fall planes were unnecessarily eight separate live draws. This is a bounded, independently testable renderer cost, not a claim that draw calls alone explain the failing performance gate.

## Production change

`bakeWaterEdgeGeometry(THREE, source, transforms)` copies each existing indexed visual mesh into shared typed arrays with its original world-space positions, correctly inverse-transpose-normalized normals, original UVs and exact triangle indices. At world creation, six foam decorations become **one** transparently blended mesh and two edge falls become **one**; ephemeral source geometries are disposed immediately and the two owned merged geometries dispose with the world. They are deliberately **live/dynamic**, never added to the opaque static cache, so they continue to overlay the animated water in the correct scene layer. No water animation, fog, original color, sprite/shape design, effect density, board, AI, deterministic tick, public snapshot, portal, food, hazard, HUD, captions, reduced-motion, WebGL fallback or recovery rule changes. No downloaded or copyrighted game assets.

## Reference critique and decision

Fixed reference: [official Snake Pass Steam gameplay and imagery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/544330/Snake_Pass/); ASTRO BOT remains a **secondary craft reference**. I inspected actual updated overview, character, near-food fixture and 16.6-second recorded motion against that reference. The original jade character remains recognizable and the unchanged near-fruit sightline is legible, but **reference wins on expressive facial anatomy, physically detailed vegetation/stone surfaces, luminous water depth, richer close-range set dressing and fluid body movement**. Our large pale regular court and relatively flat teal surrounding water retain a noticeable diorama-vs-commercial-production gap. This round did not attempt to improve art: preserving pixels while reducing known redundant draws is a targeted performance foundation, not a visual Gauntlet victory. **Self-critique only**; no separate fresh-context critic or independent R5 verdict was available. Unchanged ≥17 actual submissions gate still **FAILED** in both CI suites; sustained hardware/device/perceptual pacing, full-game visual comparison and independent signoff remain open. Never count these diagnostic submissions as display-present FPS.

**Round 19 verdict:** narrow production draw-call/resource and visual-equivalence **PASS**; overall Snake Pass visual/motion/performance/production **LOSE / OPEN**. Draft PR remains unmerged and undeployed. Next independently judgeable slice should target measured GPU water/fill or serpent cost with identical-image and unchanged-gate evidence, rather than hiding water, relaxing the fence or weakening the cadence test.

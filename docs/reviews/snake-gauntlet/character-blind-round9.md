## Largest gap in A

**The lower-jaw-to-cheek/neck connection is the most consequential visible gap.** The issue is the physical reading of the form: under the right side of the mouth, the viewer encounters a thin rim, additional pale lines, and a pointed triangular shape instead of an immediately legible rounded jaw connection. This is a stronger finish problem than a preference for one palette or expression.

## Smallest useful correction

**Round and thicken the local lower-jaw attachment at the right cheek/neck, removing the pointed wedge from its visible outline.** Keep one continuous lip edge terminating in a clear mouth corner. A small correction to that junction would improve the sense of a solid, connected head without requiring a new expression, new palette, or a wholesale character redesign.

If another small pass follows, soften the exposed outer edge of the nearer green eye surround so it blends into the side of the head instead of ending as a prominent band.

## Limits

These crops differ in camera, mouth pose, lighting, background, and visible area. B's open mouth exposes more anatomy; A's quieter pose exposes less. The comparison rewards continuity and readable thickness where visible, not the amount of visible mouth interior. B's bright lighting also limits assessment of its yellow surface detail. These images support no conclusions about gameplay readability over time, animation, deformation, controls, or motion quality.

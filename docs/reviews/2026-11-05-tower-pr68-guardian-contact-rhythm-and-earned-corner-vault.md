1. **Combat and consequences:** In the fully opted-in, source-authored guardian cap arena ONLY, actual guardian **body contact** now deals one damage rather than two. The guardian's strongly announced projectile remains its genuine **two-damage** source hit, and active shutters retain their own source damage. There are still ordinary body overlaps, i-frames, health loss, fixed-step outcomes, and losses: no shield, invulnerability, teleport, scripted victory, or removed projectile. Historical and unflagged runs, other enemies, generation, replay and collision geometry are deliberately unchanged. This gives the visible charge and projectile a meaningfully stronger consequence than incidental patrol brushing.
2. **Real cornered traversal:** The autonomous climber's existing cooldown gap controller previously stopped at a physical cap edge while the guardian's real body continued to close. When it is presently within a bounded physical contact envelope, the climber has an actual grounded **vault** option, using the existing jump physics and subsequent in-air control rather than fake dodge invulnerability. The choice is disabled if a currently active shutter intersects the present move corridor. Safe lateral retreat still takes precedence when possible; ordinary melee cooldown and actual guardian body damage still apply. Camera, articulated jump pose and airborne movement consume ordinary player motion.
3. **Truthful diagnostic instrumentation:** The **read-only** 3,200-tick source campaign now separates real guardian-body HP losses, real guardian projectiles, guardian-shutter damage and other HP losses by authoritative `player-damage` source IDs. No browser-visible telemetry, private seed, full authority or surrogate review score is exposed. This should decide the next balance/AI step from actual causal evidence instead of speculation.

## Proven defect and bounded response

Previous cosmetic combat code chose the nearest **living** opposing actor even when the actual attack was out of range, behind masonry or had just killed its victim. This could render a misleading strike or projectile at a different enemy. A ranger disarming a trap used the same generic `cast` action and could also generate a false hostile bolt when opponents were nearby. These are high-salience spectator-truth and combat-comprehension defects, not changes to the deterministic rules engine.

This iteration introduces read-only `planVisualContact(unit,snapshot)` that resolves eligible attack/cast sightlines from real authoritative map and unit snapshots, including an actor just killed this tick. It filters blocked, diagonal and out-of-range lanes and avoids a hostile target for the Warden's authentic radial cast or the Mystic's same-tick healing event. New `isDisarmCast` checks **same-tick semantic `disarm` event kind**, not localized text or health guesses. The real Three.js combat director now uses this eligible contact, shows a restrained friendly flash for a disarm, suppresses unmatchable hostile projectile cues, preserves the boss nova, and positions a smaller upright 3D melee arc near an eligible contact instead of the prior horizontal arc. The existing projectile/impact/queue budgets remain **26/24/24**; cosmetic counters are bounded. No new meshes, textures, assets, dependencies, external licenses, random draws or authoritative input are introduced.

**Scope and rollback:** `public/ai-dungeon/combat-intent-plan.js`, `facing-plan.js`, `combat-director.js`, `tests/foundation/dungeon-contact-truth.test.cjs`, `tests/browser/dungeon-authored-motion.spec.cjs`, and the focused `package.json` test script.

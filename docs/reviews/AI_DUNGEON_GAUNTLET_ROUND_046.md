## Defect and bounded improvement

Round 45 correctly kept a graphics-recovery warning visible until a post-restore WebGL frame. But source inspection found that the watchdog would clear the warning when **one rendered frame** arrived and an **old, unchanged finite authority tick** was still present. That could misreport a failed autonomous spectator feed as fully recovered. Conversely, a restored WebGL context without any subsequent 3D frame would remain misleadingly labelled `recovering` indefinitely.

The non-authoritative browser watchdog now records both the renderer-frame baseline and last public tick at restoration. Only a genuinely changed render counter **and** a changed authoritative tick clear the recovery notice. After **12 seconds** without both signals, a persistent, truthful `stalled` warning distinguishes missing 3D frames from missing authoritative snapshots. Recovery remains possible when both later advance. This adds no new effects, polling loops, textures, models, licensed assets or simulation changes. Recovery is informational, not a renderer reboot or FPS fix.

## Quality and risk review

**Specification:** The change reads diagnostics and a sanitized public snapshot. It does not mutate authoritative game state, RNG, AI, combat, collision, saves, events, server state or replay. No new assets or licensing exposure. The threshold is a spectator-status timeout, not a promise to restart WebGL or cure a blocking main-thread stall. When a browser's event loop is completely blocked, no JavaScript watchdog can update its warning until that loop resumes.

**Harsh visual assessment:** Genuine earlier-round WebGL screenshots inspected show toy-like overlapping hero/enemy silhouettes, weak weapon contact and repetitive blue rectangular stone structures, far below Diablo IV, No Rest for the Wicked and Path of Exile 2. Round 46 does not improve art and does not have a fresh exact-code screenshot inspection at this checkpoint. No independent blind matched A/B, hardware context-loss recovery test, GPU p95/p99, constrained-device endurance, fresh critic, ultracode or production proof. The coding-worker tunnel was unavailable (`Tunnel-client has not been seen for 300 seconds`).

**Outstanding lifecycle edge:** An initial startup timeout occurring during a restore that began before the first healthy render is not separately re-tested in this increment. A future test should verify recovery and startup-timeout arbitration without hiding permanent failure.

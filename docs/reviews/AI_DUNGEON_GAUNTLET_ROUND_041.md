## Verified prior failure and scope

The real Round 40 [1440×832 WebGL frame](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41238225762/artifacts/13056715554) showed widespread **near-black featureless rectangular wall tops** even though the separate decorative wall facades had textures. In the real renderer, the main world-occluding `stone` material was a globally shared flat colour `#26303d` across all instanced base walls. Adding more distant rocks, subtle limb motion or more particles would not repair that dominant field of empty dark geometry.

This increment creates a small, original masonry albedo and bump texture for the **existing real wall InstancedMesh**. It deliberately does **not** purport to replace hero models or solve the overall art-direction problem.

## Harsh spec/quality verdict

- **Preserved:** gameplay authority, seeded RNG streams, combat, hero/enemy AI, deterministic replay, pathfinding, saves, collision volumes, and offline/remote asset licence obligations were not edited. The one texture is bounded (128×128 RGBA, 65,536 base-level bytes); frame time / GPU p95 and long-duration disposal remain **unmeasured**.
- **Still failing visually:** attractive masonry print does not make a real fractured cave. Shapes remain tall cuboids, hero/enemy anatomy remains simple and overlapped, impact animation is still not clearly attributable at spectator distance, and bright cyan glows compete with silhouettes. Independent blinded Diablo IV matched-scene review, professional art critic, GPU p95, soak and device coverage are missing. The connected subagent/ultracode tunnel was unavailable; this is the author's harsh review, **not** independent sign-off.
- **Next largest blocker:** original, highly detailed hero/enemy anatomy with truly foot-planted contact-aware hand/wrist/weapon impact staging visible from the active broadcast camera. Then replace boxy surfaces with architecturally meaningful chipped geometry, improve light composition and run genuine blind reference/video comparisons.

## Observed problem and bounded plan

The genuine [Round 52 software-WebGL world capture](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41279385111/artifacts/13072733575) showed a crowded chibi-style party in a dense repetitive blue crypt. Its scene diagnostics report **7 authored textured rigs, 107 anatomical cosmetic parts and 28 actual bone-bound limb fittings**. Previous reports documented severe software-WebGL frame-delivery stalls; measuring total runtime and GPU/CPU p95/p99 remained open. Before adding more high-detail sculpting to these characters, first reduce avoidable per-character draw submissions. The incremental, reviewable target was: merge **only rigid cosmetic torso pieces sharing the same PBR finish**; preserve independent forearm/shin parts on the genuine cloned skinned bones, original textures, identity, lighting and all authoritative rules.

This is an engineering-performance prerequisite, **not** the biggest remaining *art* problem. No evidence yet proves better FPS or human preference.

## Separate specification and harsh quality critiques (self-review)

**Specification pass:** change is presentation-only and uses bounded precomputed recipes. No new asset downloads, external libraries, network calls, game RNG, state writes, dynamic lights, extra particle effects or new authoritative fields. The source models remain licensed under existing provenance. Dirty-state/material groups have finite actor lifecycle, and the merged GL buffers are registered for actor removal. Draw batching may *increase* geometry memory per character and must receive a heap/GPU-memory soak; screenshot counts alone do not establish a net system performance improvement. The fallback logs its failure and renders the original detail.

**Harsh viewer-art critique:** the near-unchanged real image still shows chibi-proportioned overlapping hero/enemy rigs, simplified face/hands/weapons, insufficient swing/contact/recovery weight, dark rectangular blue rooms and distracting accents. Compared to authentic Diablo IV, the real scene remains far behind in anatomy, material realism, terrain composition, encounter direction and combat VFX; there is no unbiased preference study. No instrumented hardware-GPU frame times or sustained p95/p99 delivery improvement, no multi-hour soak, and no production readiness validation. The connected coding-worker tunnel was unavailable, so an independent fresh-context builder/critic and ultracode **were not run**; the CI harness and self-review are not independent art criticism.

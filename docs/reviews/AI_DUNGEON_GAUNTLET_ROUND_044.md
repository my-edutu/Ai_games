## Proven priority and baseline defect

Round 43's [actual Chromium trace](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41251140585/artifacts/13061083943) showed the backend advancing while visible spectator polling froze for approximately 13 seconds. The Round 43 reactive safety switch disabled optional bloom *after* measured severe compositor stalls; it could not prevent an expensive **first** postprocessing frame. Moreover, the next [dedicated browser run 41253242570](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41253242570) failed in shard 3 because its old bloom test expected `postFXStatus='ready'` even when the runtime truthfully reported `performance-fallback` after a slow render. This failed test was preserved and diagnosed rather than dismissed as flaky.

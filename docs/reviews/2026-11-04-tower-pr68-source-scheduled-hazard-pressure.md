## Real experience problem and linked playable intervention

During rapid autonomous ascent and portrait capture this left little advance physical language. Eight instanced pieces per hazard, up to eight hazards in one draw (four on low graphics); it yields to a genuine guardian charge, stale packets, terminal results, death and replay-reset. Reduced-motion mode holds a static recognizable posture. Graphics-context rebuild, full scene dispose, and diagnostics are integrated into the real `scene.mjs`, not a model preview or decorative /tower/gauntlet screen.

The existing opt-in audio engine now emits one quiet warning timbre only on an **observed** adjacent same-floor tick transition from unarmed countdown zero to a genuine charge, near a living climber. First frames, floor changes, restarts, duplicate ticks and competing guardian telegraphs cannot generate its cue. Existing active-hazard and combat audio remain in place.

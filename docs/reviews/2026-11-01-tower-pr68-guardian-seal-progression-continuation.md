## Problem this cohesive vertical slice addresses

The real playable 3D Tower already shows the guardian's fixed-step charge, articulated maul/shield, world-space threat lane, defensive autonomous decision, and physically grounded camera. But defeating a guardian offers only a sudden software floor transition, with no legible monumental world response. A separate isolated model/render flourish would not solve this. This slice binds the **same actual observed guardian encounter** to the tower's visible physical gate and its opt-in sound, without modifying gameplay physics, replay or AI and without adding HUD paragraphs.

## Actual live-game modifications

1. The genuine guardian cap has a separate **original 3D inset bronze seal** with structural pillars, lintel, two sliding leaves and a physical faceted lock. It is positioned *inward* of the collision-aligned annular walking corridor; the cap mesh and its top remain exact to the authority. The seal is a separate floor-owned scene group, not an invisible collider or an overlay. No guardian in the public source → no decorative gate.
2. The seal reflects **real remaining guardian HP**, progressively parting the leaves under actual attacks; full clearance only follows an observed inactive guardian. This is important because the authority can legitimately advance from a final boss hit directly to the next floor **on the same fixed tick**. Waiting exclusively for an inactive-guardian snapshot would produce an animated opening that never appears in ordinary play. Each render derives the target from a source snapshot, and the bounded presentation interpolation preserves reduced-motion stable meaning, stale rendering, moving-cap transforms, quality switching, restart and graphics rebuilding.
3. A matching original opt-in synthesis provides subdued physical feedback on actual guardian health decreases. Its three-note passage cue recognizes either an observed same-floor active→inactive transition **or an actual next-floor advancement from a previously observed live guardian**, without hallucinating a kill on replay, new run, unrelated floor, or disconnected renderer. No external copyrighted sound.
4. The separate portal fixes a genuine regression discovered by the initial [integrity run](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41337445987): attaching full-height architecture to the collision-aligned cap group broke an existing geometry invariant that the deck top is exact. The gate was restructured as an independently transformed sibling, excluded from rigid static batching; the original platform top and ray clearance are left unchanged. New tests inspect its physical model, two-leaf progression, hit-point response before instant floor advance, stable transforms, source integrity and restart/quality cleanup, plus source-bound audio dedupe/mute.

**Safety/performance intent:** one original finite mesh assembly on a real guardian cap, no shadows from decorative leaves, no unbounded particles or audio voices, no new authoritative state/clock, no new external services, no UI text. Actual supported-device draw-call, frame-tail and visual acceptance have **not** been measured.

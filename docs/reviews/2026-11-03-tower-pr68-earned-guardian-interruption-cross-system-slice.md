## Actual implementation
- Existing autonomous combat can choose an earned legal Pulse Shot while the live guardian charges, respecting safe footing, incoming-projectile priority, cooldown, facing and real vertical projectile lane. No new omniscient observation or control override.
- When a **real player-owned projectile** overlaps a **living guardian whose telegraph still has future ticks**, authoritative combat removes that pending windup, holds actual guardian movement and attack for **10 deterministic 20Hz ticks** and grants a minimum **18-tick** recovery cooldown. A real already-fired projectile remains live. Ordinary shooters, misses, deaths and off-timing impacts **do not** earn interruption. Contact damage remains active, so standing inside the enemy is not free safety. New event: `guardian-windup-interrupted`, with source id and remaining charge ticks. No RNG calls, wall-clock gameplay timing or new hidden reward.
- Only opt-in **live snapshot** projection carries `staggerTicksRemaining`. Default legacy render snapshots retain their key set for historical replay; restored older enemy state without the optional field behaves normally. The original authoritative snapshot and checksums still own truth.
- The actual existing original 3D guardian skeleton now shifts weight into planted knees, drops the maul, dims its visor and lowers its center during a *source-observed* interruption. Reduced-motion viewers receive a stable readable pose. Stale/terminal source cannot manufacture it. Existing pooled world-space armor-impact mesh remains visible for the bounded stun; the red danger-warning ring is absent while the guardian cannot fire. No additional HUD, unbounded particles, new renderer, lights or textures.
- Opt-in original audio produces a restrained material/tone release only after an adjacent same-run and same-floor real charge→stagger transition. Repeated frames, restarts, mute and unrelated HP changes cannot fabricate the cue; existing voice limits remain.

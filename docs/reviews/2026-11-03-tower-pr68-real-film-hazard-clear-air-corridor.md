## Systemic traversal repair — current playable code

The existing same-slice pilot already grounded its pre-jump braking in observed motion and chose a collision-safe airborne deck lane. Two actual physics/AI mismatches remained:

1. **Wrong-way dash risk:** tactical airborne dash eligibility used distance to the deck midpoint, independently from braking direction and **the real player facing**. Fixed-step physics executes the dash along `player.facing`, which can still point away from the target when steering coasts or brakes. A long fast dash can send the autonomous climber away from the real safe landing surface.
2. **Nominally safe but dangerous touchdown:** a usable platform corridor can overlap an ACTIVE spike/other hazard. Air steering ignored the presently observed hazard geometry and could settle into a lethal or damaging section even when a clear section of the same deck existed.

The new *live tactical* controller uses immutable present public target/hazard data to subtract actual active hazard x-ranges expanded by the player body's half-width and safety clearance, chooses a genuinely open interval of the currently observed platform, and aims/retains lateral momentum toward that landing lane. If no usable interval exists, it **does not invent** a safe future landing. The airborne boost is permitted only on a sufficiently distant real lane while rising, with current stamina and dash cooldown available, no opposite horizontal momentum or active brake request, and it explicitly uses that direction as its same-step movement so existing authoritative physics faces before dashing. No future hazard phases or moving-deck trajectory, collision rewrite, extra draw calls, new HUD or randomness.

Existing grounded `settling-launch`, airborne `settling-landing`, articulated skinned character poses, world-space route stones and optional sound remain the connected experience. The live source now names genuine hazard-clear ordinary steering and hazard-clear braking distinctly. [The original 3D route stones](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/blob/feat/tower-transformation-gauntlet-20261101/public/infinite-tower-climb/visuals/route-intent.mjs) consequently converge toward the **same present body-clear physical deck lane** *only when that authoritative source intent explicitly names it*, including boosting and settling. The guidance retains its one depth-tested instanced draw and source-owned moving-deck echoes, gives no predicted landing or false promise to older/historical intent, clears on stale/terminal/restart and respects reduced motion. Original skinned air-brake/dash poses and existing audio remain physically tied to these actions. Historical `tactical:false`/v1 path is unchanged for pinned legacy replays. New seven-source contract tests at `tests/phase2/tower-air-survival-corridor.test.cjs` exercise a real generated deck, physical same-tick forward dash from previously opposite facing, disallowed boost when momentum opposes/stamina is insufficient, near-lane anti-overshoot, actual active hazard clearance, inactive/full-blocked hazard behavior, immutability and deterministic decisions. Additional instanced-route source tests verify hazard-clear target, real-braking summary, prior-source midpoint fallback, inactive hazards and stale-source clearing.

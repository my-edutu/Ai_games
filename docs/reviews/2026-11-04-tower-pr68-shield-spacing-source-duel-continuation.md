## Experience defect and continued transformation

The preceding source-authentic [nine-job real-game capture](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41740541619) recorded desktop 960×600 and portrait 390×844 normal-speed autonomous ascent, danger and outcome/restart. The current portrait 69-point samples put the climber inside the 78% safe frame, but **actual comparable fight beats are not yet aligned or blindly reviewed**. A different systemic issue remains in the authentic guardian phase: the real 22-tick low-health guardian shield can make the autonomous climber stand close to the raised shield, doing nothing until the timer clears. A timed shield by itself is not meaningful action.

We therefore continued the **same OPEN** ascent → guardian counterplay → HP-unlocked world mechanism → genuine terminal/restart vertical slice with a shared source-backed decision/action/feedback beat:

- On an **actual** opted-in guardian's observed raised shield, a grounded climber with a present valid cap/deck corridor and no immediately available *earned* aligned Pulse Shot opens modest physical space instead of spending a guaranteed blocked melee cooldown. The decision reads only current guardian guard ticks and enemy/deck positions. No future patrol, invulnerability, shield-break power, fabricated hit or fixed-step clock is added.
- The existing safety controller still rules out going over a support edge; observed active spikes veto any counterstep through their present collision interval. Unrelated real hazard and projectile reactions pre-empt this encounter choice. The guard spacing applies only to **nearby** guardian contact; a remote guardian cannot stall a climb.
- During a real telegraphed charge the guardian's core is exposed and the prior higher-priority brace/counter-shot or evasive response owns the action; a legitimately earned Pulse Shot is still available through the previous combat system. Historical replays/configuration without `guardianSecondWind` never enter this branch.
- The already skinned 3D expedition climber performs a lower guarded footwork silhouette from the **same observed AI intent**. Reduced-motion retains a restrained physical stance, not an effect that hides the decision. Opt-in audio adds one quiet transition cue; muted playback remains complete without sound. No new HUD copy, mesh, shadow light, server event or material is created.

## Systemic gap: physical threats were not part of the climber's visible decisions

The preceding game rendered an original, depth-tested 3D jaw shutter for a genuine server-scheduled trap for twelve 20-Hz ticks and an optional charge sound. But the **autonomous climber had no safe-footing response before actual activation**: it could appear to run through its own cinematic warning. This work connects three real layers of the same game, rather than introducing UI, fake heroics or another isolated shader:

1. **20-Hz truth and AI:** The tactical pilot now explicitly opts into the *same* source-derived `chargeTicksRemaining` observation as the 3D scene. Its historical-v1 observation, original public snapshot and default checksum shape remain unchanged. It never scans future RNG, changes a hazard activation schedule or grants a hit.
2. **Grounded counterplay and descending landings:** A living climber on a present verified support deck reacts only to a nearby currently published **1–8 tick** warning. Existing projectiles, active traps and imminent enemy shots retain priority. The pilot steps away from the actual hazard body with existing next-step platform-edge limits; in the final **1–3 ticks**, if still physically inside the hazard's horizontal hit zone, it may use an **ordinary** jump. During a real descending approach, the final **1–6 tick** warning can redirect toward a body-clear existing landing lane, without claiming the trap's future state or creating a new deck.
3. **Physical 3D presentation and sound:** The real narrow-portrait camera can include the *currently charged* hazard that elicited the pilot's `evading-hazard` decision instead of focusing only after activation. Existing physical shutters, optional observed-source audio and the climber's defensive pose now have a matching player decision. Genuine guardian, result and airborne landing are still hard-priority.

No new HUD prose, asset copying, collider, health, score, network event, RNG call, or hardcoded victory is introduced. Renderer owns no authority. Behavior is O(hazards) with bounded fixed-step reaction and deterministic ID sorting. Current trap stage has eight instanced parts per nearby hazard, up to eight hazards (four on Low), one draw and no shadow or dynamic light. Reduced-motion retains static source warning geometry. These limits are source budget, **not real hardware FPS evidence**.

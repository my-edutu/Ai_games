## Actual existing evidence and largest remaining quality gap
Real native 390×844 normal-speed guardian footage from [original-host run 42007783650](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/42007783650), artifact `13408653952` (candidate `c5ded7ef`), visibly placed a huge wheel and foreground supports across the living climber and guardian at approximately 7 seconds. Sampling source camera projections falsely suggested readable hero/guardian/gate framing: **NDC and clear tower core do not measure opaque scenery in actual pixels**. The same-time frozen-baseline portrait video already showed a different floor, so this is a **builder-side causal observation**, not a matched A/B preference or experiential acceptance.

The previous source-led continuous gate camera `b7a55046` did not address a specific remaining occluder: the clockwork rotor's **opaque dial, clock housing and decorative case** did not share the existing protective fade of its spokes and hub. Even if the gear softly yielded to live combat, its static large dark plate could still fill the phone frame.

## One connected actual 3D-game improvement
[Playable world change `6ee3e488`](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/6ee3e488c2d9a0ee775dc1cf73806f407bbe0e14) expands the pre-existing actor/guardian/landing camera-to-world sightline treatment from the decorative clock gear to its **entire floor-owned non-colliding case**. Both the large central dial and the two side gear backings now use separately cloned, precompiled alpha materials, associated with each existing gear. The same source-observed camera corridor, ray tests and depth-aware projection determine when the full sculpted decorative clock face fades; it becomes solid again afterward. Geometry, original rotating mechanisms, colors, rim and materials remain physically modeled.

The fix intentionally excludes **true platforms, ledges, moving-deck drive geometry, shutter teeth/warnings, boss, source-authentic HP-operated portal, combat telegraphs and collision**. No gameplay simulation mutation, new camera/renderer pass, extra mesh, new AI decision, HUD element, network call or random state. Shadow casting is disabled **only for the decorative case when newly camera-responsive**, avoiding opaque shadows from an alpha-faded non-gameplay dial. Original character, arena, portal and authoritative 20Hz control remain intact. Each floor owns and disposes its private face materials through the pre-existing floor-material lifecycle.

A focused [source-level contract test `cfeb568a`](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/cfeb568a97e9017f6690d04c17a2bdebfd48b08d) checks private material ownership, coordinated translucency/depth restoration, absence of geometry deletion, and that the actual world invokes the same guardian/screen corridor rather than a fake clip or future prediction. This test is engineering hygiene, **not** visual validation.

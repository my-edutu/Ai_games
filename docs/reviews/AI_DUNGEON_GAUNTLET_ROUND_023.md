## Harsh quality verdict

This is an original, bounded identity accent on an existing low-detail humanoid, **not** an artist-quality creature sculpt, advanced skin/cloth shading or motion-captured combat. Static regalia follows the cosmetic root rather than each animated bone, so shoulder/cuirass clipping during high-amplitude clips must be inspected. The photographed scene is still dark and boxy, and neither Diablo IV art parity nor production readiness is remotely demonstrated. Browser gates passed, but independent visual judgement **rejects full Gauntlet**: the new boss is more recognisable, yet the encounter is partly obscured by a large black foreground wall and the underlying animated characters remain low detail. The occlusion is a concrete P1 spectator-readability defect to address next; silhouette deployment is not final art acceptance.

**Next highest blocker:** fix the visible black foreground-wall cutaway around real boss combat and strengthen camera-aware boss/party framing, then inspect moving clips for horn/armor intersection. The long-term gap remains original detailed hero/enemy anatomy, rigged combat motion, material shading, richer nonrectangular architecture and independent matched blind comparisons. Keep PR #49 draft; do not merge.

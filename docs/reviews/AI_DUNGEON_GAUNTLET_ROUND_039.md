## Evidence-led selection, independent increments

Round 38's real 1440×832 and mobile browser footage proved that the characters' upper arms can animate but original hero/enemy anatomy, contact poses and locomotive leg motion remain tiny and unconvincing from the spectator camera. This iteration improves **actual imported skinned leg bones**, not 2D art or an offscreen mock model. It is a bounded motion increment rather than a complete character art overhaul.

1. **Acceptance first:** four new foundation tests committed to `tests/foundation/dungeon-rig-attachments.test.cjs`, selecting genuine left/right upper-leg, lower-leg and foot joints while rejecting hands, toes, twists and wrong-side bones; verifying finite mirrored, settled and reduced-motion gait, teleport rejection, and real renderer wiring. Tests landed in [801acc3](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/801acc3ff2e1e07dbe099417646f0024b80767b4), with a regex-escaping correction [75463a9](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/75463a9f16dad82a049fda58a6e9c2d230eb26cb), **before** implementation. GitHub Actions for the test-only heads were cancelled by subsequent commits; **there is no archived contemporaneous RED CI result** and one must not be invented.
2. **Pure bounded gait planner:** [e7f5e05](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/e7f5e05efa5f395742bc486d6da09a3d6966cec5) adds `selectLegBone`, `advanceRigGait` and `planRigLegGesture` to `public/ai-dungeon/rig-attachment-plan.js`. Phase advances from **actual displayed actor travel distance**, not a wall-clock running cycle. On no movement, weight decays to zero; invalid numerical input, >0.32m single-frame jump and reduced motion reset the local overlay. Mirrored additive thigh/knee/ankle rotations remain within 0.235/0.165/0.075 radians. All calculations are deterministic, presentation-only and allocation-light.
3. **Actual GLB renderer integration:** [4222142](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/4222142fbf9bb6fda921120e0196d83862f490b9) binds only real lower-limb bones on cloned, existing locally vendored CC0 skinned meshes, reverses the last cosmetic quaternions **before** each imported AnimationMixer update, then applies the next bounded overlay. No-bone/offline fallback safely skips. Actor cleanup clears gait references; renderer diagnostics now include `rigGaitBones` and `rigGaitPeakRad`. No new geometry, external assets, materials, remote services, physics, collision, combat, AI, seeded RNG or save schema.
4. **Actual browser verification:** [1f0958c](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/1f0958c41a554be1b962e1dc4e42743e416384ab) adds a genuine Chromium scenario derived from a position transition in real `DungeonRuntime.step()` authority snapshots. It waits for textured skinned models and requires at least four **bound real leg joints**, observed >0.07 and ≤0.30 radians of motion, zero page errors, visible live heroes and bounded joint counts. It captures a real WebGL scene PNG and real-browser video; it is not proof that the audience perceives convincing physical foot planting.

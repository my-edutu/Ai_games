## Biggest systemic weakness and approach

Previously inspected, normal-speed source-pinned [desktop and phone gameplay](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/actions/runs/41358227934) exposed authentic guardian health loss while the boss, gate and winch left the phone image. The preceding HP-coupled armor, portal, winch and physically aligned guardian stage only matter when an actual viewer can track their cause and effect.

The next cohesive presentation defect was **cross-tower camera motion**: a valid 3D camera can start on one side of the cylindrical tower and aim toward another valid camera pose on the opposite side. The old Cartesian `position.lerp` cuts *through the tower's opaque core* during that transition, turning genuine attacks and unlocks into an obscured wall shot. Similarly, the old `lookAt.lerp` can stare through the core while the camera moves safely outside. A candidate gate could also win camera-selection points because it fit projected screen bounds even when the solid tower lay directly between camera and gate.

Both updates reuse existing vectors and compose against the existing source-derived camera target; reduced-motion can settle immediately on that same target.

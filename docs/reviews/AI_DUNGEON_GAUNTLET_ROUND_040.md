## Proven gap and smallest defensible increment

The genuinely rendered Round 39 movement-specific 1440×832 Chromium frame showed a broad near-black cavern skyline over a small overlapping party, with boxy stone walls below. Its 19.03/255 mean pixel luminance and 85.4% of pixels under luminance 35 are only **single-frame diagnostics**, not a cross-seed aesthetic baseline or an unambiguous brightness regression. The old presentation placed 34 distant narrow cylindrical skyline pillars and tiny bright crowns beyond the playable 19×19 square; they did not provide a convincing natural cave boundary.

The smallest isolated correction is to replace these distant scenery-only columns with **original non-playable rock ridges**, without touching heroes, authority or procedural walkability. This is not a hero-model or full art-system replacement.

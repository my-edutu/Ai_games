## Problem identified from actual Round 41 captures

Round 41's real Chromium WebGL captures still showed small, overlapping imported 3D character silhouettes amid repetitive rectangular masonry. Imported shoulder and distance-paced leg motions existed, but the character combat arms largely remained rigid from shoulder to hand, without a deliberate secondary elbow wind-up / extension / recovery phase. Adding particles or HUD copy would not repair that missing visible articulation. This round fixes one bounded, independently testable subpart of that larger anatomy problem, not character-model quality generally.

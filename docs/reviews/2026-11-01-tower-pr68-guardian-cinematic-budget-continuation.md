## Why this is ONE continuing cross-cutting playable slice

Earlier source-pinned motion inspection found inadequate climber/guardian scale and difficult-to-read physical danger, while software-renderer candidate frame-submission intervals were significantly slower than frozen baseline; neither those intervals nor source logic prove native-device FPS. The current slice attacks **both**: the 3D guardian now pre-announces an actual fixed-tick combat decision across stage/camera, planted rig, world-space warning and opt-in sound, and the low-quality world preserves convincing far distance without the opaque/transparent remote-city overload. This is a cohesive in-game transition from ordinary ascent to high-stakes encounter with no extra HUD paragraphs, not an isolated new effect or a new quality PASS.

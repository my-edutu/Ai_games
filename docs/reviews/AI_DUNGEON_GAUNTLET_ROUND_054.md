## Evidence-based gap and scope

The prior genuine Round 53 Chromium/WebGL screenshot shows the same seven very stylized, overlapping CC0 character rigs and dense, blocky blue environment. A prior screenshot and the software-WebGL frame-delivery logs also document severe stalls. Before adding unbudgeted polygons or shaders, source review uncovered redundant CPU work in the running character renderer: **every rendered frame** recomputed 2-panel, 8×6 cloth deformation and vertex normals and marked *all* corresponding position buffers dirty, even after a transient action had fully settled, or when reduced motion made the whole garment static. This is **proven repeated work**, **not** proof that cloth caused the frame stalls or that FPS improves.

This bounded increment caches only an actor's **exact cloth pose** by a pure presentation key: changing idle/walk/attack/cast/hurt/guard ages still deform on every distinct frame, but settled post-attack, post-cast, post-hit and post-guard cloth, reduced-motion cloth and unknown rest poses do not rebuild/upload identical geometry. A new action or change to the reduced-motion setting invalidates the key; disposal clears it. The renderer keeps the original fabric forms, color, real GLB rigs, author animations and existing resource owners. Diagnostic counters are capped per actor; no timer, random stream or gameplay property was added.

# Infinite Tower Climb — source-true physical damage and survival continuation
**Date:** 2026-11-02 · **PR:** #68 draft · **Game source:** [`ff33d32`](https://github.com/my-edutu/Ai_games/commit/ff33d3242c0deca5ac7f578a9e707d1be2e2bd70) · **Experience gate:** NOT EVALUATED

## Largest addressed presentation gap
The existing game had an original skinned explorer, an HP-operated guardian and lock, landing/route guidance, moving-platform weight, five atmospheric regions, and a physical ending. Yet the protagonist's own health attrition and recovery were principally short pose/HUD information. With no persistent physical injury, genuine dangerous autonomous decisions could read as costless, especially with minimal in-game HUD. This changes the *running model*, not a static landing page: persistent damage and recovery now carry consequences across ascent, combat and the next route choice.

## Cohesive game-source work
- Two original articulated ceramic breastplate leaves are built into the existing skinned climber skeleton. They expose the explorer's **existing original blue torso underlayer and copper trim** as HP decreases: a persistent physical shape change, not another health-bar color. Actual public `player.health / player.maxHealth` opens the leaves continuously, and authentic recovery closes them. Fresh entry and restore rehydrate directly from HP. No arbitrary "damage" script or bonus pickup is invented.
- A short bounded torso/helmet/forearm recoil is permitted only on a **new, consecutive, same-run, same-floor source tick** showing a genuine health decrease. Duplicate snapshots and changed run/floor or rolled-back ticks cannot fabricate impact; existing guardian attack, defense and authoritative hurt/terminal poses take priority.
- Actually depleted `stamina / maxStamina` makes quiet standing posture subtly heavier, without changing speed, AI, gravity or viewer HUD. Real action supersedes fatigue.
- Both new armor hinges share the existing rig/material and workshop disposal. Reduced-motion playback retains the persistent damaged *shape* but suppresses the additional recoil/fatigue animation. No extra particles, lights, collision, result timing, random draws, platform changes, sound prompts or copied reference assets.
- The scene passes the existing authoritative floor with run token/tick and exposes a source-derived `climberIntegrity` diagnostic, expressly **not** claiming raster visibility or actual frame rate.

## Selected proven gap and independent increments

Round 37's genuine Chromium combat captures proved that existing GLB hero/enemy silhouettes and rig animation lacked visible, believable weapon-hand anticipation, impact and recovery at spectator scale. This round deliberately worked on **actual imported upper-arm bones**, not a 2D mockup or merely moving a whole model.

1. **Test/selection contract:** three new foundation assertions identify genuine left/right upper-arm joints and reject fingers, hands, forearms, shoulder/clavicle and twist bones; require finite, safe, deterministic shoulder gestures for melee, ranger draw, casting, hurt, guard, and reduced-motion. Pure selector/planner implemented in `public/ai-dungeon/rig-attachment-plan.js`.
2. **Renderer integration:** in `public/ai-dungeon/model-assets.js`, find bones from **cloned** locally vendored CC0 skinned GLBs. Layer small quaternion gestures **after** the real `AnimationMixer` frame; undo the prior layer **before** the next mixer update so animation tracks lacking arm keys cannot accumulate rotation. Bind on asset readiness; no eligible bones means a safe no-op; the usual actor retirement clears the extra references. Existing textured rigs, imported clips, hand-bound bow/staff equipment and separate cape deformation remain the source of art. Diagnostic fields `rigGestureBones` and `rigGesturePeakRad` expose what the real renderer actually exercised.
3. **Genuine Chromium assertion:** `tests/browser/dungeon-authored-motion.spec.cjs` takes a witnessed attack transition from the true `DungeonRuntime.step()` public snapshots, then checks **at least three actual bound upper-arm bones**, peak observed angle **above 0.15 rad and at most 0.43 rad**, real authored attack motion, capped effects, texture readiness and zero page errors. This is an integration assertion, not a perceptual art-quality judgment.

No AI, pathfinding, collision, damage, RNG, authoritative snapshots, map, saved-game state, or remote dependencies changed. Shoulder gestures are render-only, frame-bounded and set to rest under reduced motion. No new third-party assets, license changes or materials.

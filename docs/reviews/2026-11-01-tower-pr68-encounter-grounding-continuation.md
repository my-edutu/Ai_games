## Chosen systemic gap and coordinated playable changes

The prior real-game self-review found small climber/guardian silhouettes in desktop and portrait danger scenes, weak physically grounded contact, and candidate software-render submission tails slower than the frozen reference. A route-oriented view can keep a far landing and a nearby opponent equally prominent, shrinking both. An actual landing could be animated but had no shared world-space contact readable on low quality.

1. **Stage real encounters:** The existing 3D follow camera now temporarily prioritizes an *observed* enemy/hazard/projectile when the real AI intent is fighting, bracing or evading. Camera fitting avoids reserving space for a far route landing during that immediate moment, targets larger actor fractions (.225 desktop, .205 portrait), retains actual 3D threat/world geometry, and keeps the existing route beacon. Nothing changes world physics, AI authority, collision, replay or the original camera-smoothing/lens behavior.
2. **Make real platform contact visible:** One reusable depth-tested 3D ring rests on the actual deck top, triggered only when two consecutive same-run/same-floor public snapshots show a fast airborne descent into the authoritative standing/contact state. Its scale, fade and subtle optional camera settle vary with observed descent velocity. No invented contact is generated when a snapshot gap exceeds eight authority ticks, the run changes, the player is dead, or stale transport is active; reduced motion keeps a stable ring.
3. **Make authentic touchdown audible:** Muted-by-default synthesized contact now weights biome-sensitive low-level texture and pitch to observed descent speed, with voice limits/audio ownership unchanged. The animation, in-world visual and opt-in audio are a single source-grounded movement beat, not three unrelated cosmetic effects.

# Content and Live Operations

## Content Classes

- deterministic rule and configuration profiles;
- procedural grammar and validator packs;
- authored rooms, floors, arenas, courses and events;
- themes, sprites, models, shaders, UI and fonts;
- music, stems, ambience and SFX;
- safe names, copy and localization;
- interaction effect definitions;
- seasons, challenges and record categories.

## Content Lifecycle

Proposal → design and rights review → schema validation → generator and game tests → performance, accessibility, audio and content-safety review → versioned pack → staging and canary → promotion → monitoring → deprecation, archive or rollback.

Every item records stable ID and version, owner, game and mode compatibility, deterministic impact, licence and provenance, asset budget, localization and accessibility, moderation class, rollout window, fallback and rollback.

## Live Operations Rules

- Content cannot execute arbitrary code.
- Active content is immutable and hashable.
- Deterministic-impact changes create new rule or content versions and record categories where needed.
- Event schedules are declared and cannot secretly force a winner or failure.
- Rare spectacle has cooldown plus sensory and performance budgets.
- No live-operations urgency bypasses provider, moderation, security, privacy or licence review.
- Expired seasons retain replay and record compatibility or declare archival limits.
- Operators can disable a content or effect pack independently.

## Renewal Without Manipulation

Renewal comes from validated topology, mechanics, objectives, themes, choices, AI strategy and authored event grammar. It does not come from hidden odds, forced deaths, fake records or misleading audience influence.

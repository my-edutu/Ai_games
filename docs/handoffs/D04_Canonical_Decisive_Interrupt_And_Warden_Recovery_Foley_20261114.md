## Observable problem and truthful correction

D04's strict audio gate already verified sword attempts, actual victim HP loss, releases, Warden warnings, block/dodge and earned counters. But high-priority `interrupt`, `defeat` and `recovery` recipes were still allowed as raw sounds when `requireVerifiedContact=true`. A malformed or stale cue could play a decisive interrupt/defeat/recovery stinger without the public planner's corresponding semantic event; this is particularly misleading in real combat.

The new `verifiedDecisiveOrRecoveryFoleyCue()` is stateless and checks **actual planner-shaped semantic evidence**:
- Public anonymous `interrupt` and `defeat` require their matching kind/audio/visual, verified `authority-event` proof, and explicitly **no** actor, target, from or to. Event prose is never parsed as proof of an attacker or successful strike.
- Authored Warden `opening` requires `recovery` audio, `opening` visual, `authority-opening-recovery-window` proof, a bounded actor ID, a valid observed public cell, and no invented victim or strike destination.
- All three must also pass the existing canonical `run:floor:tick:kind:actor:target` event ID and scene/tick validation. Strict mode refuses raw standalone recipes without using a voice or event-history slot. Neutral standalone WebAudio audition mode intentionally remains permissive.

A well-formed recovery cell is not independent proof of its *correct* position: the read-only planner/controller owns authoritative public snapshot validation. No inferred damage or impact attribution is introduced.

## Dominant D04-owned correctness defect selected

`public/ai-dungeon/physical-feedback-visuals.js::emit` previously accepted a `visual:'launch'` or `visual:'cast'` with a valid live ranger/caster and target even when its cue `proof` was `observed-hp-drop` or entirely guessed. Conversely `visual:'hit'` accepted a nominal `observed-hp-drop` with an unrelated kind or no target identity. This adapter ingress could turn a genuine *injury observation* into an unproven *weapon release*, or mint a localized contact fragment without a known injured victim. This defect was verified by a production-visuals native regression; it is **not claimed to have appeared in previously captured Chrome footage**.

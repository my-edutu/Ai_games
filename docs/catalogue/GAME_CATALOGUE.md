# Game Catalogue and Portfolio Roles

## Current Truth

The documentation and architecture foundation is complete. Runtime implementation has not started, and no game is production ready. The next bounded execution target is Autonomous Snake Phase 1.

## Portfolio Thesis

The portfolio is not twelve unrelated games. It is one autonomous livestream platform expressed through twelve spectator fantasies. Each title must make its goal legible within ten seconds, expose persistent progress, produce renewable dramatic patterns, accept bounded audience influence, recover from common failures and restart automatically.

## Recommended Release Order

1. Autonomous Snake — establishes shared contracts and operations.
2. Marble Survival — proves mass participation and deterministic physics.
3. AI Maze Escape — proves partial observation and content oracle.
4. Infinite Tower — proves action physics, streaming rooms and builds.
5. AI vs 1,000 Floors — proves long campaign persistence.
6. AI Battle Royale — proves many-agent competition and camera direction.
7. AI Escape Room — proves bounded reasoning and model integration.
8. Endless AI Dungeon — combines content, combat, economy and choices.
9. AI Zombie Survival — adds persistent base and large hordes.
10. AI Traffic Experiment — proves reconciled city-scale flow.
11. AI Ant Colony — proves large swarm and ecosystem state.
12. Tiny AI Civilization — final synthesis of persistent agents, economy, history and diplomacy.

The order may change only through an evidence-backed dependency or capacity decision. It is not a marketing ranking.

## Cross-Game Modes

- Standard autonomous run;
- Chat vs AI;
- no-audience resilience mode;
- seasonal or challenge configuration;
- clean-feed broadcast;
- accelerated headless simulation;
- deterministic replay and highlight;
- canary and production health modes.

## Completion Definition

A game is complete only when its own R5 criteria pass. Reusing a production-ready shared package shortens implementation but does not transfer gameplay, balance, accessibility, provider, soak or canary evidence automatically.

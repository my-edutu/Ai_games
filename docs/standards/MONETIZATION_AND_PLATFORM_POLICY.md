# Monetization, Gifts and Platform Policy Standard

## Purpose

Enable sustainable livestream monetization without turning gifts, memberships or chat into undisclosed gambling, guaranteed outcomes, unsafe manipulation or platform-policy risk.

## Allowed Value

Subject to current provider and regional policy, support may grant:

- capped vote weight or an additional eligible ballot;
- nomination of one pre-authored eligible option or category;
- cosmetic acknowledgement, theme, banner or history entry;
- an eligible bounded effect request that may be accepted, queued, rejected or expire;
- next-run or season challenge and cosmetic choice;
- non-competitive persistent cosmetic or community progression.

## Prohibited Value

- guaranteed victory, survival, death, elimination, record or puzzle solution;
- direct winner selection or targeted harm/protection of a named competitor;
- unbounded power based on exact payment amount;
- secret odds, rerolls, rubber-banding or outcome manipulation;
- wagering, cash-equivalent prize or paid chance mechanic without separate legal and platform review;
- raw payment details in game state, public output, analytics or logs;
- bypassing moderation, cooldowns, caps, regional policy, accessibility or safety;
- misleading acknowledgement claiming a viewer caused an outcome not supported by authoritative events.

## Provider-Neutral Entitlement

The Audience Gateway converts verified provider events into minimal evidence: provider and reference, entitlement type or band, verified and reversal state, time, region and policy version, and idempotency key. Exact financial data and billing identity remain outside the game.

## Effect Requirements

Every monetizable effect declares plain-language outcome bounds, eligibility, entitlement mapping by provider configuration, cooldown, conflict, cap, queue, expiry, record category, reversal or refund behavior, moderation, accessibility, deterministic command, audit, acknowledgement and emergency disable.

Payment entitles the disclosed opportunity, not guaranteed application or result. The public UI must show accepted, queued, applied, rejected or expired status appropriately.

## Fairness and Records

Paid and free events share the same safety and eligibility layer. Competitive games cannot target identified participants. Assisted modes and records identify their interaction configuration. Standard records may exclude strong aids according to transparent rules; historical records are not rewritten invisibly.

## Reversal and Failure

Duplicate, retry, reconnect and recovery cannot apply an effect twice. Reversal creates a new audit event and follows effect-specific policy; completed gameplay is not secretly rewound. If provider, entitlement or audit truth is unavailable, paid-eligible authoritative effects reject or defer. Autonomous gameplay continues.

## Moderation, Privacy and Regional Policy

Public names and text are optional, sanitized, bounded and moderated. Viewer references are scoped, tokenized and retained only for purpose. Regional and provider feature flags disable unsupported effects. Policy configuration and provider documentation are reviewed at R3, R4 and before every production launch because requirements may change.

## Experiments and Metrics

Measure accepted, applied, rejected, expired and reversed events; duplicate application; contribution distribution; effect frequency and stacking; fairness and complaints; moderation and accessibility; provider errors; and product outcomes. Revenue or watch-time cannot override integrity, safety, privacy, fairness or platform-policy guardrails.

## Launch Gate

A monetized interaction launches only with current provider verification, threat, privacy and moderation review, production-equivalent idempotency and reversal tests, visible outcome bounds, safe disable, operator and runbook ownership, and no P0 or P1 finding.

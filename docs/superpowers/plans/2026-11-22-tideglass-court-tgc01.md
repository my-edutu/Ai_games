User-approved one-time implementation milestone following *Tideglass Visual and Level-Design Blueprint v1.0*. The hourly Game 2 Gauntlet is paused by the user. This plan does not resume it.

## Scope and baseline

Baseline: `a97eb68fe73aab44e3645b58e7352937e07e8b68`, open draft PR #53, branch `feat/maze-3d-gauntlet`. Preserve the procedural levels and the accepted terminal camera. No merge, deployment, force-push, hidden map disclosure, artificial AI move, screenshot crop, or threshold relaxation.

## Acceptance

- Both actors share actual reciprocal same-tier cells at contact. No floor spans a sealed passage or undisclosed cell.
- Gallery access is blocked by real authority until the earned counter. Pressure, guardian warning, counter, gallery key, return, matching gate and escape occur naturally in one run.
- Complete escape ≤200 ticks and counter-to-key ≤120 ticks. Existing procedural pacing remains intact.
- Full physical models remain inside ±0.95 NDC; existing stricter per-beat silhouette and pressure thresholds remain untouched.
- Original six-reset geometry budget <100 and texture budget ≤4 remain intact; the new fixture is also exercised through six actual run identities.
- Original 44px controls, public-only rendering, keyboard/accessibility, reduced motion and fallback remain intact.
- Four-view unaltered evidence supports an independently judged improvement in court readability. This is not a claim of Captain Toad parity.

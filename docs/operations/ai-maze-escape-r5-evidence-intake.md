# AI Maze Escape R5 Evidence Intake

## Purpose

Move AI Maze Escape from its verified **R4 / 88 out of 100** software-candidate state to an evidence-backed **R5 / 100 out of 100** production-ready verdict for one exact frozen deployed candidate. CI fixtures, synthetic timestamps, copied reports, and evidence from another source/config/content/deployment identity are not substitutes.

## Entry criteria

Before collection begins, freeze and approve:

- exact 40-character source SHA;
- immutable deployment artifact or image digest;
- platform, game, deterministic, snapshot, event, and provider-adapter versions;
- configuration, content, asset, and feature-flag hashes;
- production region, host, GPU, encoder, audio, and capture identity;
- provider/effect/public-text disable state;
- release, on-call, security, broadcast, accessibility, and product owners;
- exact previous-version rollback identity and fresh-run compatibility boundary.

A material source, configuration, content, asset, provider, or deployment change invalidates affected evidence and restarts endurance/canary clocks.

## Provider evidence

### YouTube

- user OAuth authorization and refresh/revocation;
- current live-chat event families;
- reconnect, duplicate, stale, malformed, rate-limit and outage behavior;
- Super Chat/membership reversal and entitlement uncertainty;
- privacy-safe identity, no raw payment or private provider payload in authority/public output.

### Twitch

- EventSub webhook and/or WebSocket authentication;
- forged/stale signature rejection;
- welcome/keepalive/reconnect/revocation;
- at-least-once delivery deduplication;
- cheer/subscription/gift reversal, rate-limit and outage behavior;
- privacy-safe scoped viewer references.

Each record must include the exact release checksum, production-equivalent environment, credentialed status, owner, collection and expiry time, source classification, primary-evidence digest, and all required checks.

## Safety, accessibility, and content evidence

External reviewers must cover:

- threat model, authorization, secrets, input validation, dependency and supply-chain risk;
- identity/payment minimization, retention, deletion, access, and public-output privacy;
- sanctions, abuse, brigading, public text, paid-event fairness, reversals, and emergency disables;
- desktop/mobile/low-bitrate comprehension, keyboard/operator access, color-safe patterns, reduced motion/flash, captions, muted comprehension, and audio calibration;
- provenance and licences for every shipped visual/audio/content asset.

## Endurance and canary stop conditions

Immediate failure or rollback is required for any replay divergence, unsolvable content, hidden-information leak, duplicate authoritative effect, private exposure, unauthorized control, unavoidable paid capture, record corruption, failed restore, unresolved black/frozen/wrong-scene/silent output, crash loop, platform-policy breach, or unbounded resource slope.

The reviewer receives the frozen manifest and primary evidence, verifies candidate identities and elapsed provenance, inspects representative median/tail/failure runs, confirms zero open P0/P1 findings, lists accepted P2 risks, and submits a signed content-addressed verdict.

# AI City Traffic Experiment R5 Evidence Intake

The release validator accepts external evidence only when every record is bound to the frozen release-manifest checksum. CI fixtures and synthetic elapsed timestamps remain blocked.

Required evidence:

- production-reference capacity samples with attested hardware, p99 budgets, resource slopes, and headroom;
- real elapsed 72-hour endurance evidence with at least 73 samples and zero replay divergence, duplicate effects, restore failures, or private exposure;
- current credentialed YouTube and Twitch authentication, reconnect, duplicate, reversal, outage, and rate-limit checks;
- externally signed security, privacy, moderation, accessibility, audiovisual, assets, and supply-chain attestations;
- all mandatory runbook drills in production with an independent witness;
- a real seven-day canary with at least eight ordered samples and threshold compliance;
- an independent exact-candidate review with no open P0/P1 findings.

Evidence that is stale, self-attested, unsigned, candidate-mismatched, synthetic, or incomplete must remain blocked. Accepted records are retained beside the release validation JSON and candidate deployment manifest.

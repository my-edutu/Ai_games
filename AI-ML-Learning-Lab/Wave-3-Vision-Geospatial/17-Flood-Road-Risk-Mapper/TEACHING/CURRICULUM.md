# Curriculum

1. Load/align DEM, rainfall and roads.
2. Derive elevation/slope/water-distance features.
3. Build a rule-based risk baseline.
4. Create spatially blocked evaluation.
5. Train a boosted risk model.
6. Map errors and data/report coverage.
7. Add uncertainty and freshness states.
8. Build point/road/route API.
9. Validate on historical flood events.
10. Produce safety-aware release/runbook documentation.

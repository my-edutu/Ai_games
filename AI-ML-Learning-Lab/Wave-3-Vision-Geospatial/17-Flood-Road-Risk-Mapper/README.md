# 17 — Flood & Road Risk Mapper

Build a geospatial risk mapper combining rainfall, elevation, drainage/water proximity, historical flood reports and road networks to estimate route/road flood exposure.

**Primary skills:** GIS, raster/vector processing, geospatial ML, graph/road networks, risk scoring, uncertainty, map UX.

**Final deliverable:** versioned risk-map pipeline/API that communicates uncertainty and data freshness and supports—not guarantees—routing/safety decisions.

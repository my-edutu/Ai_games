# Phase 1 — Problem Discovery and Safety Contract

Define static susceptibility vs near-term flood risk, spatial resolution, freshness and user actions. Never label a route “safe” solely because predicted risk is low.

**Build:** risk taxonomy, users, data-freshness contract, safety wording, metrics.

**Learn:** hazard vs risk, uncertainty, safety-critical map communication.

**Exit:** product uses risk bands and freshness, not absolute safety claims.

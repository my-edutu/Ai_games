# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Stress missing weather stations, cloudy satellite periods, extreme seasons, unseen regions and crop-calendar shifts.

**Build:** robustness matrix, fallback baselines, uncertainty intervals, feature-driver summaries and spatial error analysis.

**Learn:** missing sensors, distribution shift, spatial fairness/access issues.

**Exit:** low-data areas carry wider uncertainty or explicit unsupported status.

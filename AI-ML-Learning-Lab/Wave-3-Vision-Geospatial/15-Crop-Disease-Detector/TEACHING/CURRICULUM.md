# Curriculum

1. Audit a crop-image dataset for leakage/duplicates.
2. Build a transfer-learning baseline.
3. Report per-class metrics/confusion matrix.
4. Compare augmentation strategies.
5. Fine-tune two backbones and ablate changes.
6. Calibrate confidence and add rejection threshold.
7. Build blur/light/background challenge set.
8. Export/serve model and verify preprocessing parity.
9. Conduct simulated/real expert field review.
10. Produce model card with supported scope.

# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Stress blur, poor lighting, multiple leaves, soil/background changes, healthy-but-damaged plants and unsupported crops.

**Build:** image-quality gate, OOD/low-confidence fallback, challenge set, Grad-CAM or equivalent qualitative audit.

**Learn:** OOD inputs, shortcut learning, visual explanations.

**Exit:** unsupported/uncertain images do not receive confident disease labels.

# Phase 2 — Data Contract and Governance

Define image, crop, disease label, severity, location/context, capture device, annotator and provenance fields. Separate research/public data from field-collected data.

**Build:** schema, labeling guide, licensing/consent, train/test grouping rules.

**Learn:** dataset provenance, label quality, image leakage.

**Exit:** duplicates and near-duplicates cannot leak across splits.

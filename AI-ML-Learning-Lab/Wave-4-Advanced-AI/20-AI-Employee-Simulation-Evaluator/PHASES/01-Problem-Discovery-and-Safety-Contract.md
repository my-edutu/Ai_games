# Phase 1 — Problem Discovery and Safety Contract

Define simulation roles, competencies, artifacts, decisions, time pressure and evaluator outputs. Scores must assess demonstrated simulation performance, not personality or real-world employability beyond validated scope.

**Build:** competency model, rubric, evidence rules, non-goals, appeal/review path, metric tree.

**Learn:** performance assessment, construct validity, rubric design.

**Exit:** every score requires observable evidence and defined competency.

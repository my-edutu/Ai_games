# 12 — Medication Adherence Predictor

Build a supportive risk model that identifies patients who may benefit from adherence assistance. It must not shame patients, infer non-adherence as moral failure, or alter treatment without clinical review.

**Primary skills:** temporal classification, calibration, missing data, intervention targeting, fairness, privacy, healthcare ML.

**Final deliverable:** calibrated outreach-support prototype with supportive-action policy, monitoring and clinical governance gate.

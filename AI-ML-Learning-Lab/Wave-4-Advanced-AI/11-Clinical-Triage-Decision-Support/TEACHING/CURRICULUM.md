# Curriculum

1. Write narrow intended-use + hazard analysis.
2. Construct patient/time-safe data split.
3. Train transparent calibrated baseline.
4. Compute sensitivity/specificity/PPV/NPV under prevalence shifts.
5. Compare boosted model and ablations.
6. Audit site/subgroup calibration and missingness.
7. Build uncertainty/abstention + clinician override UI.
8. Design fail-safe monitoring/change control.
9. Draft retrospective/silent validation protocol.
10. Produce clinical safety case clearly stating evidence gaps.

# Phase 4 — Baseline and Evaluation Harness

Implement clinically interpretable baseline and evaluate sensitivity/specificity, PPV/NPV at realistic prevalence, calibration and decision-relevant thresholds.

**Build:** baseline, confidence intervals, calibration, subgroup/site metrics.

**Learn:** prevalence dependence, calibration, clinical utility.

**Exit:** threshold choice is tied to explicit missed-case/escalation costs.

# Phase 1 — Problem Discovery and Safety Contract

Define one narrow triage outcome, intended clinical user, setting and escalation protocol. Never position this as autonomous medical diagnosis/treatment.

**Build:** intended-use statement, clinical workflow, hazard analysis, human-review requirements, metric tree.

**Learn:** clinical decision support, sensitivity/specificity trade-offs, safety cases.

**Exit:** a qualified clinical stakeholder must own intended use before real deployment.

# 14 — Maternal Health Follow-up Risk

Build a support model to identify pregnant/postpartum patients at risk of missing recommended follow-up so care teams can offer reminders, transport/navigation or outreach. Never use it to deny or deprioritize care.

**Primary skills:** temporal health classification, calibration, fairness, missing data, intervention design, privacy.

**Final deliverable:** clinically governed supportive-outreach prototype with high-sensitivity evaluation, equity monitoring and human oversight.

# Phase 10 — Production Readiness and Handover

Conduct final evidence-based review across quality, safety, security, privacy, reliability, cost, documentation, and ownership.

**Build:** model card, data card, runbook, SLOs, incident/rollback plan, release checklist, known-limitations register, handover notes.

**Learn:** production gates, SLO/SLA concepts, model governance, operational ownership.

**Exit:** release evidence is reproducible, owners are named, limitations are explicit, and deployment can be reversed safely.

# Phase 9 — Pilot, Human Review, and Impact Validation

Run a controlled pilot with real or representative users. Compare model ranking with baseline and human expectations; capture disagreement instead of treating clicks as unquestioned truth.

**Build:** pilot protocol, feedback instrument, disagreement taxonomy, qualitative review, online/offline metric comparison, incident log.

**Learn:** A/B testing, selection effects, implicit feedback bias, qualitative evaluation, guardrail metrics.

**Exit:** pilot evidence supports deployment or identifies concrete changes; no unresolved P0/P1 safety issue remains.

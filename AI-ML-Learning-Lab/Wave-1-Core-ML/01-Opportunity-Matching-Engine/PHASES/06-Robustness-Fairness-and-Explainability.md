# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Stress incomplete profiles, noisy opportunity text, unseen categories, duplicates, and distribution shifts. Compare outcomes across justified demographic or geographic cohorts without using protected attributes as shortcuts.

**Build:** robustness suite, fairness report, reason codes/explanations, adversarial cases, uncertainty/abstention policy.

**Learn:** subgroup evaluation, disparate impact concepts, SHAP/permutation importance limits, counterfactual thinking.

**Exit:** known weak cases are documented and high-risk uncertainty triggers fallback rather than silent ranking.

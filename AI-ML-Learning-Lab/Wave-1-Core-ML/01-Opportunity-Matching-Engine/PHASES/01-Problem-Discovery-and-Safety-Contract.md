# Phase 1 — Problem Discovery and Safety Contract

Define who is being matched, what an opportunity contains, what “good match” means, and which decisions must remain with the user. Separate hard eligibility from soft relevance. Identify harmful failure modes such as hiding viable opportunities, amplifying historical inequality, or inferring sensitive traits.

**Build:** problem statement, user journeys, ranking objective, non-goals, safety boundaries, decision matrix, initial metric tree.

**Learn:** ranking vs classification, precision/recall trade-offs, proxy labels, human-in-the-loop design.

**Exit:** stakeholders can explain the target decision, acceptable errors, protected attributes, and success metrics without referring to implementation.

# Known Limitations

The included training and pilot evidence is synthetic and does not establish real-world utility, fairness or causal impact. Text semantics are represented through curated tags rather than a production embedding pipeline. Geographic and opportunity taxonomies are intentionally small. Feedback-loop bias, stale opportunity data, provider-side selection effects and regional internet/access inequities require live-product evaluation.

The model ranks only opportunities that pass explicit eligibility; ambiguous provider rules should trigger human review rather than inferred eligibility.

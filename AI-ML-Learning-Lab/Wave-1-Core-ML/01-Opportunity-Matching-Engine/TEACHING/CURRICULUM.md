# Curriculum

1. Implement a rules-only matcher and explain every weight.
2. Compute Precision@K, Recall@K and NDCG by hand on a tiny example.
3. Build a temporal train/validation split and demonstrate leakage with a deliberately bad feature.
4. Compare cosine similarity, boosted ranking and an embedding-based candidate generator.
5. Perform an ablation study and error taxonomy.
6. Evaluate at least three meaningful cohorts.
7. Write user-facing reason codes that do not overclaim causality.
8. Serve the model through an API and load-test it.
9. Simulate drift and trigger an alert/rollback decision.
10. Defend the final model in a short model-review memo with evidence and limitations.

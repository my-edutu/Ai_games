# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Stress uncommon job titles, non-standard resumes, career changers, sparse profiles, multilingual phrases and inflated job requirements.

**Build:** robustness matrix, confidence thresholds, source-highlight explanations, cohort analysis.

**Learn:** OOD inputs, confidence, explainability, bias in job descriptions.

**Exit:** unsupported inferences are suppressed and evidence is visible to users.

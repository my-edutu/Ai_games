# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Audit performance by facility, appointment type, age bands and other approved cohorts. Stress new patients, missing contact data and workflow shifts.

**Build:** subgroup report, robustness tests, uncertainty/fallback, non-stigmatizing reason codes.

**Learn:** healthcare bias, calibration by subgroup, uncertainty.

**Exit:** supportive outreach does not systematically exclude or burden a group.

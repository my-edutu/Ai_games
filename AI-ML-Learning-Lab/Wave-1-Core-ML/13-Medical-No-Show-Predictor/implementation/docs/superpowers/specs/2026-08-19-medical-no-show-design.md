# Medical No-Show Predictor Design
Appointment-level risk support only. Allowed actions: reminders, transport help, scheduling outreach and capacity planning. Prohibited actions: care denial, cancellation, deprioritization, punitive treatment, or diagnosis inference from the score.
Use only information available before the appointment. Protected traits are excluded from model features. Sparse/stale cases abstain. Every response exposes model/data/intervention-policy versions, reason codes, uncertainty and human-review metadata.

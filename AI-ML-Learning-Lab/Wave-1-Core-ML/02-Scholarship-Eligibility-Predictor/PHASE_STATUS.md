# Project 02 — Phase Status

## Phase 1 — Complete
Decision taxonomy separates eligibility, needs-review, and predictive suitability. Guarantee claims are prohibited.

## Phase 2 — Complete
Versioned scholarship policy and applicant schemas validate provenance and trace every policy result to source/version.

## Phase 3 — Complete
Deterministic representative dataset generation, temporal split, quality metrics, and snapshot hashing are implemented.

## Phase 4 — Complete
Exact rule engine and transparent logistic predictive baseline are covered by hand-authored edge cases and classification/calibration metrics.

## Phase 5 — Complete
Candidate comparison includes calibrated interaction logistic and calibrated boosted tree. Selection uses PR-AUC, Brier score, and recall guardrails; threshold tuning protects high-cost cohorts.

## Phase 6 — Complete
Missing/ambiguous GPA and predictive inputs abstain; fairness reporting and non-sensitive feature audit are implemented.

## Phase 7 — Complete
Typed FastAPI endpoint returns policy eligibility/policy trace and optional suitability separately; ineligible applicants receive no suitability score.

## Phase 8 — Complete
Policy and model registries activate/rollback independently; monitoring distinguishes policy-data and model failure domains; audit logging is append-only.

## Phase 9 — Complete (representative evidence)
Hand-authored blind review reaches 100% policy accuracy and comprehension copy passes the estimate/non-guarantee gate with zero P0/P1 findings. This is not real-user evidence.

## Phase 10 — Engineering release candidate complete
Model/data cards, policy runbook, monitoring, security/privacy, ownership, rollback, release checklist, benchmark and pilot evidence are reproducible. A real-user controlled pilot remains an external production-evidence gate.

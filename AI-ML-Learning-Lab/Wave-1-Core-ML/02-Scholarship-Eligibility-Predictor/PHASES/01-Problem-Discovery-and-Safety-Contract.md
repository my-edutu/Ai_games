# Phase 1 — Problem Discovery and Safety Contract

Separate deterministic eligibility from probabilistic prediction. Define prohibited claims: never tell a user they will win funding merely because a model score is high.

**Build:** decision taxonomy, policy boundary, false-positive/false-negative impact analysis, metric tree.

**Learn:** classification vs rules, calibration, asymmetric error cost.

**Exit:** every output is labelled eligibility, unknown, or predictive suitability with appropriate caveats.

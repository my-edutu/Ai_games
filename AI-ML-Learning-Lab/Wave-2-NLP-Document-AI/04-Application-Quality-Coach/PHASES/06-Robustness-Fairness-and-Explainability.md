# Phase 6 — Robustness, Fairness, and Explainability

Test different English varieties, concise writing, non-native styles, code-switching, adversarial instructions and missing context.

**Build:** robustness suite, bias review, evidence highlighting, uncertainty/refusal rules.

**Learn:** linguistic bias, prompt injection, calibration of subjective outputs.

**Exit:** style differences are not mistaken for lack of substance and unsupported claims are refused.

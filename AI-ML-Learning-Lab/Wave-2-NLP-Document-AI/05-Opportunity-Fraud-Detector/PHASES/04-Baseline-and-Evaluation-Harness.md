# Phase 4 — Baseline and Evaluation Harness

Start with transparent rules and a linear text/metadata classifier. Focus on precision at review capacity and recall of severe cases.

**Build:** heuristic baseline, PR-AUC, precision@review-budget, calibration, false-positive review set.

**Learn:** rare-event metrics, operational thresholds.

**Exit:** baseline establishes reviewer workload and missed-risk trade-offs.

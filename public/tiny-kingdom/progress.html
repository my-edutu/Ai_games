Independent acceptance gates

What the build currently demonstrates

  • Actual rendered 3D geometry (terrain, houses, trees, river, villagers), movable camera.
  • Villagers navigate autonomously, work toward assigned role destinations, consume and produce resources.
  • Settlement develops new cottages and citizens over simulated time.
  • Fixed-step simulation loop, playback controls, world reset and story log.
  • Real headless Chromium run: initial state loaded, simulation advanced, pause held simulation time, no page errors.

Harsh visual critic — still failing

  • Largest gap: characters are simple assembled primitives, not individually modelled and animated humans.
  • Terrain, architecture, lighting and natural assets lack the surface detail and material quality of Manor Lords.
  • No advanced global illumination, shadows, weather system or cinematic effects.
  • NPC cognition and relationships are scripted utility routines, not a rich society with memory and consequential choices.
  • No blind A/B test against matched official benchmark frames has passed.

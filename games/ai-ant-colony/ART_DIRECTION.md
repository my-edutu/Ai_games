# Art Direction

## Target

A nature-documentary 2.5D slice of earth, not a dashboard or scientific visualization. The world must remain understandable with the HUD hidden.

## Visual language

- Organic brown, clay, root, moss and weathered vegetation tones.
- Natural role differentiation through body scale, head/abdomen proportion, mandibles and behavior rather than bright team colors.
- Surface depth from grass, stones, debris, puddles, atmospheric lighting and foreground occlusion.
- Underground depth from soil strata, roots, moisture, rocks, chamber shadow and deep-earth falloff.
- Pheromones are contextual and temporary; observer mode may expose detailed routes.
- VFX are dirt, dust, rain, moisture, threat pulses and restrained organic impacts. No neon sci-fi language.

## Broadcast composition

The ecosystem owns the entire viewport. Status and narrative components float as compact translucent overlays, with a clean-feed mode that removes them. The current CSS target is approximately 90% world / 10% informational UI.

## Readability test

At representative 1080p framing, an ant should read as a six-legged insect with antennae/body orientation, the queen as an in-world biological entity, predators as distinct spiders/beetles, and the nest as layered earth with real chambers/tunnels rather than colored cells.

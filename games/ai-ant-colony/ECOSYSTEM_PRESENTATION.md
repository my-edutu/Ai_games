# Ecosystem Presentation

The browser maps authoritative state to a living surface/underground cross-section.

## Surface

Stable hashed dressing adds grass, stones and organic debris. Existing food cells become physical seed/leaf/remain-like sources. Rain produces surface water response and rain streaks; heat produces shimmer/dry presentation. Seasonal palettes alter vegetation and light without changing simulation rules.

## Underground

Soil is divided visually into topsoil/subsoil/deep bands, with roots, rock, water and tunnel/chamber shadows. Queen and brood occupy the real nest-center region. Active diggers emit bounded soil particles; tunnel-completion events receive short presentation pulses while authoritative tile mutation remains the source of truth.

## Colony behavior

Food carriers visibly transport items. Soldiers/diggers/scouts/nurses differ by morphology. Contextual food/alarm/excavation pheromone routes appear only when relevant recent events justify them, unless observer mode is explicitly enabled.

## Threats

Authoritative spider and beetle entities receive species-specific silhouettes. Threat elevation changes framing and queen-danger emphasis rather than relying on a red bar alone. More advanced bite/grapple choreography remains a polish target and must not invent combat outcomes.

# VFX and Audio System

VFX is restrained: emissive instrument pulses, hazard wall pressure, light-practical accents, physical mechanism motion, solved-state lift/rotation, compartment reveals and a small bounded dust field. The final door physically moves during escape.

Audio is generated locally with WebAudio and contains no provider/runtime dependency. Semantic cues cover inspection, clue discovery, item pickup, combination, puzzle solve, hazard transition and escape. A low ambient hum provides room tone when browser audio is available. `muted=1` disables cues; captions remain the complete visual alternative.

The implementation does not make progress depend on hearing. Autoplay restrictions may keep WebAudio suspended until browser/OBS policy permits playback; this is a presentation limitation, not game truth.

# Art Direction — The Cipher Vault

The existing Cipher Vault identity is retained: a premium high-security archive/control room using painted steel, dark stone, aged wood, brass mechanisms, paper clues, cyan instrument light and restrained amber practical light.

The room is intentionally coherent rather than a mixture of Victorian, laboratory and fantasy motifs. Geometry is architectural and functional: vault door, desk, shelving, cabinet, archive fixtures and puzzle modules. Cyan indicates observed/active systems; amber indicates physical mechanism focus; green indicates authoritative solved state; red is reserved for real hazard/time pressure.

Material families are represented separately even in the lightweight renderer: steel, painted steel, brass, wood, paper, glass/emissive instrument surfaces and stone. The style avoids glossy sci-fi clutter, floating icons and large dashboard cards. The default frame targets approximately 90% environment / 10% interface.

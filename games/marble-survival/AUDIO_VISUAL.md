# Marble Survival Tournament — Audio and Visual Direction

The visual language is a dark tournament arena with luminous course geometry and bright marble silhouettes. Each competitor is identified by number, icon, pattern, outline, and trail shape in addition to palette. The arena remains dominant; HUD cards replace one another rather than accumulate.

Semantic priority is integrity/result → lethal danger/qualification → milestone/comeback → audience acknowledgement → contact → ambience. Camera modes are overview, pack, leader, danger, finish, replay, and ceremony. Audio uses capped semantic voices and adaptive countdown/race/pressure/finale/victory/elimination/intermission/maintenance states. Every critical sound has visual/caption equivalence. Reduced-motion, reduced-flash, muted, colour-safe, clean-feed, and quality tiers preserve meaning.

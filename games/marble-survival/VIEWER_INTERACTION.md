# Marble Survival Tournament — Viewer Interaction

Viewer roles are favourite supporter, tournament strategist, and cosmetic patron. Launch choices are `wind-vote`, `gate-tempo`, `shield-orb`, `cheer-pulse`, `theme-vote`, and `next-arena`. Inputs are normalized outside the game and enter as fixed-choice privacy-safe requests.

The game validates schema, fixed option, rate/cap, idempotency, state eligibility, cooldown/conflict, and safe scheduling. Effects cannot write finish, elimination, or champion fields. Paid evidence never bypasses safety or moderation. Raw text, display names, payment details, and provider IDs do not enter authority or public snapshots. Provider outage leaves a complete autonomous tournament.

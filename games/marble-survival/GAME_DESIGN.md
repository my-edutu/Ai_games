# Marble Survival Tournament — Game Design

## Core Rules

Marbles begin at a common safe spawn band and autonomously steer toward checkpoints and the finish. A round qualifies its declared quota; hazards can eliminate; timeout ranks unresolved competitors by authoritative progress, vertical position, then stable ID. Qualifiers alone enter the next seeded arena. The championship first valid qualifier becomes champion.

## Round Grammar

1. Seeding Sprint: wide lanes, bumpers, introductory contact.
2. Gate Gauntlet: moving sweeper timing and route choice.
3. Hazard Circuit: pits, wind, recovery pressure.
4. Final Four: compact high-contact qualification.
5. Championship: mirrored duel geometry and one decisive slot.

## Progression and Closure

Primary progress is survivor count plus round. Secondary progress is checkpoint/finish progress. Every round emits start, checkpoint, contact, qualification/elimination, resolution, and next-round events. Tournament result emits champion, record category, recap source events, intermission, and deterministic restart.

## Fairness

Personality changes approach—not result entitlement. Generated championship geometry is mirrored. Audience power effects stop at the decisive boundary and move records to an assisted category. Technical failure is separate from game loss.

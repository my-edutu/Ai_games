# Art Direction

Marble Survival is a broadcast miniature-motorsport spectacle: physical, engineered, legible and cinematic rather than neon UI around circles.

## Priorities
- Arena occupies the visual majority; UI is supporting information.
- Marbles are polished volumetric spheres with readable color identity, restrained highlights and grounded contact shadows.
- Machinery is visibly constructed with housings, hubs, rails, supports and finish gantries.
- Round themes vary deck, trim, accent and atmosphere without implying unimplemented physics.
- Championship presentation uses darker structure and warm metallic accents to feel climactic without changing fairness.

## Avoid
Flat circles, CSS perspective, constant glow, excessive particles, giant dashboards, fake physics materials, fake destruction and decorative hazards that contradict authority geometry.

## Current round palettes
Seeding Sprint: neutral motorsport steel; Gate Gauntlet: industrial graphite/orange; Hazard Circuit: dark heat-treated metal/red-orange; Final Four: cool precision steel/blue; Championship: charcoal/brass/gold.

# VFX and Audio System

## VFX
The WebGL renderer currently emits bounded deterministic presentation particles for official elimination, shield-recovery, qualification and champion events. Particle directions are generated from event sequence numbers, not ambient randomness. Quality presets cap active visual particle draw counts.

Contact shadows ground marbles. Threat/recovery/focus states use restrained rings so event readability does not depend on text.

## Audio
The existing browser runtime retains event-driven Web Audio tones for qualification, elimination, recovery and champion moments. Audio remains presentation-only and cannot influence authority.

## Quality rules
Strong effects are reserved for consequential events. Constant collision particle spam is intentionally avoided. Cosmetic material choices do not imply different physics.

## Incomplete audio scope
Continuous rolling sound, collision impulse-scaled sound, machinery loops, spatialized arena ambience, glass/water/ice surfaces, adaptive tournament music and a dedicated final-round score are not yet implemented. They remain explicit follow-on work.

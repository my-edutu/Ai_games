# Production Readiness — AI Battle Royale

## Readiness levels

- **R1:** deterministic headless match foundation and replay checksums.
- **R2 gameplay:** autonomous combat, progression, generation and balance evidence.
- **R2 broadcast:** responsive browser source, accessible HUD, semantic audio/VFX and output self-test.
- **R3:** bounded, idempotent, privacy-safe audience interaction and provider-degradation behaviour.
- **R4:** verified persistence/recovery, supervisor, chaos, operations, capacity evidence and rollback machinery.
- **R5:** exact deployed candidate passes production provider verification, independent review, witnessed drills, real 72-hour endurance and real seven-day guarded canary.

## Stop-ship conditions

Ambient authoritative randomness; replay divergence; invalid map counted as loss; illegal action; duplicate elimination/effect; raw user/provider/payment data in state or overlay; targeted paid damage; unbounded history/queue; corrupt snapshot continuation; crash loop; black/frozen/silent output without recovery; inaccessible critical state; missing rollback owner; any open P0/P1.

## Truthful status rule

A self-authored software review may conclude `BLOCKED / R4 candidate`. Only independent exact-candidate evidence may conclude `PASS / R5 / productionReady: true`.

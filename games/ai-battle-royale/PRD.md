# Product Requirements — AI Battle Royale

## Problem

Always-on autonomous streams need a premise that is immediately understandable, produces renewable conflict and attachment, resolves regularly, and remains reliable without an operator. Many battle simulations fail because agents feel scripted, information becomes unreadable, or monetized audience actions undermine fairness.

## Product outcome

Deliver a last-agent-standing stream where a new viewer understands survivors, danger and current leader within ten seconds; autonomous contenders exhibit distinct legal strategies; every outcome can be replayed; and the system continues through optional provider or presentation failure.

## Functional requirements

- Generate a connected deterministic arena for twenty-four unique spawn points.
- Advance authoritative rules on fixed logical ticks with named RNG streams.
- Run the complete match without human input or remote inference.
- Support four visible archetypes and at least four weapon/resource behaviours.
- Resolve line-of-sight combat, cover, ammunition, healing, movement conflicts, loot, supply drops and zone pressure deterministically.
- Emit typed semantic events for presentation, replay, analytics and operations.
- Automatically display result, intermission and next deterministic match.
- Provide a responsive OBS-compatible browser source with accessible HUD, captions, mute and reduced-motion paths.
- Accept only normalized fixed-choice audience influence with idempotency, logical windows, bounded weight and audit.
- Persist versioned snapshots and replay inputs; restore only after checksum, compatibility and invariant validation.
- Detect gameplay, render, audio, persistence and provider degradation independently.
- Produce evidence-gated R1–R5 assessment without self-certifying external production evidence.

## Success measures

- 100% same-seed checkpoint/final checksum equality in deterministic tests.
- 100% generated arenas pass connectivity, spawn, reachability and bounds checks in the declared seed corpus.
- Zero illegal actions, duplicate eliminations, duplicate audience applications or technical failures classified as game losses.
- At least three dramatic match patterns appear in seeded campaign review.
- New-viewer goal/progress/danger comprehension passes representative mobile capture review.
- Critical state remains understandable muted and in reduced-motion mode.
- Snapshot restore equals uninterrupted replay checksum.
- Synthetic software validation cannot promote above R4.

## Non-goals

Human player controls, real-money prizes, viewer-targeted damage, unrestricted chat commands, raw text-to-speech, photoreal character production, remote-model dependency and secret outcome balancing are excluded.

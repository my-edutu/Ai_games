# Product requirements

## Viewer promise

Watch twelve independent contenders adapt and fight for the final safe ground. Goal, surviving contenders, storm danger and the observed competitor must be understandable within ten seconds. Results must be earned by the actual rules.

## Owned deliverables

Authority owns legal actions, movement, loot, weapons, health, storm and outcomes. Presentation owns world geometry, articulated models, cameras, HUD, semantic effects and optional audio. Development tooling owns review evidence and remaining-gap tracking.

## Acceptance

- Complete matches resolve to truthful winner/draw and restart without viewer action.
- Same seed/version/configuration reproduces outcomes; accepted checkpoints reproduce continuation.
- All twelve roster members render original articulated bodies and current equipment/state.
- Primary information and camera controls fit desktop and 390px phone widths.
- Failure to fetch state holds the last verified view and shows reconnect status; invalid authority quarantines and cannot display a fabricated win.
- Independent critics inspect actual captures and behavior. The commercial reference bar remains unchanged until sufficient evidence supports a win.

## Exclusions

No audience influence, paid boosts, provider credentials, account data or remote AI inference is part of this implementation. The generic future platform GameModule contract is not claimed fully integrated; the current runtime follows the implemented standalone stream-host conventions.

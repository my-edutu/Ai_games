# Phase 6 — Production Validation and Launch Governance

**Target:** R5 machinery  
**Initial status:** Not implemented on the feature branch.  
**Exit rule:** Every criterion must have reproducible evidence plus separate specification and quality reviews.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Frozen candidate/evidence manifest and requirement traceability.
- [ ] Full tests, campaign, stream, chaos and nondeterminism evidence ingested.
- [ ] Assessor cannot promote synthetic/self-authored evidence to R5.
- [ ] External 72-hour endurance, seven-day canary and independent review remain explicit gates.
- [ ] Final review has zero open P0/P1 before merge eligibility.

## Review ledger

The phase ledger is updated only after focused tests, affected regressions, evidence capture, specification review and engineering/viewer-experience review. P0/P1 findings block progression.

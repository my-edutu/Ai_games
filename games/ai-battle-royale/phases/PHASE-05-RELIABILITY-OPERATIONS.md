# Phase 5 — Persistence, Recovery and Operations

**Target:** R4  
**Initial status:** Not implemented on the feature branch.  
**Exit rule:** Every criterion must have reproducible evidence plus separate specification and quality reviews.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Versioned snapshots, replay manifests and restore checksum equality.
- [ ] Corruption/incompatibility quarantines instead of silent continuation.
- [ ] Independent tick, render, audio, persistence and provider health probes.
- [ ] Finite restart breaker, safe scenes and bounded resource lifecycle.
- [ ] Chaos evidence, runbook, rollback matrix and ownership handoff.

## Review ledger

The phase ledger is updated only after focused tests, affected regressions, evidence capture, specification review and engineering/viewer-experience review. P0/P1 findings block progression.

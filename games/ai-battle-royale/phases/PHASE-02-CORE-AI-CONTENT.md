# Phase 2 — Autonomous Combat, AI and Progression

**Target:** R2 gameplay  
**Initial status:** Not implemented on the feature branch.  
**Exit rule:** Every criterion must have reproducible evidence plus separate specification and quality reviews.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Four archetypes choose only validated bounded actions.
- [ ] Simultaneous combat, deterministic movement conflicts, cover, loot and supplies.
- [ ] Declared shrinking-zone and no-progress escalation.
- [ ] Causal terminal results, intermission and automated continuation contract.
- [ ] Seeded campaign records balance tails and no illegal actions.

## Review ledger

The phase ledger is updated only after focused tests, affected regressions, evidence capture, specification review and engineering/viewer-experience review. P0/P1 findings block progression.

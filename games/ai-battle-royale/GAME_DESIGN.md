# Game design

The arena is a 120-unit coastal outpost. Seven solid research structures and ten cover objects provide route decisions and occlusion. Contenders deploy around a seeded ring, move at fixed logical steps and see only enemies within their observation radius and clear line of sight.

Four weapon families have different magazines, fire intervals, effective ranges, accuracy and damage. Agents begin with health, shields and limited medkits. Visible loot can replenish resources or change equipment. Reloading and healing consume logical time. Damage is accumulated before elimination so mutual eliminations remain possible.

The storm contracts toward a safe focal area and eventually makes indefinite survival impossible. A match resolves when at most one living agent remains. Zero survivors is a draw. A three-second deployment and eight-second intermission frame each cycle; match identifiers advance with each restart.

The warm sandstone / teal sea / ivory architecture palette differentiates the world from competitors. Chartreuse marks spectator selection and important information; labels and numbers preserve meaning without color. Atmosphere and effects never change hit probability or game rules.

Sampled pacing is recorded in the review ledger rather than promised as a fixed duration. Records and long-term progression are outside this build.

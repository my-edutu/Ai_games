# Autonomous contenders

Policies are deterministic local systems. No model service participates in the hot loop. Agents receive visible enemies and visible loot, then select between immediate storm escape, reload, healing, combat positioning, looting and search. Personality variation uses named seeded streams.

## Boundaries

Observation range is capped and shooting repeats weapon-range and line-of-sight validation. Movement uses shared swept rectangle geometry. Visibility-graph paths are finite, with deterministic tie breaking. Stuck detection and path replanning are bounded; they do not silently teleport through structures. Every resolved damage, elimination and outcome is generated by authority.

## Public explanation

The HUD exposes short intent labels, health, equipment and eliminations. It never exposes hidden chain-of-thought. Public snapshots omit internal paths, planning state, RNG state, seed and medkit inventory.

## Evidence

`tests/battle-royale/runtime.test.cjs` exercises reproducibility, sampled path/solid invariants, full rounds and restart. The headless command samples additional seeds. These are bounded engineering checks, not proof of statistical balance or human-level tactics.

# Audio and Visual Direction — AI Battle Royale

## Visual hierarchy

Survivors and zone pressure dominate. Contenders use role-specific geometry, outline, nameplate, health/shield bars and restrained intent markers. Obstacles, cover and loot remain subordinate; background and particles degrade first.

## Camera

The default is a stable full-arena tactical view with bounded focus emphasis. No camera motion changes authority. Reduced-motion mode removes impulses, interpolated trails and nonessential transitions.

## Semantic feedback

Priority: integrity/result → lethal danger/elimination → final-circle/milestone → audience acknowledgement → hit/pickup/movement → ambience. Common hits are merged and deduplicated; lower-priority cues cannot mask danger or result truth.

## Audio

Dependency-free synthesized WebAudio provides low-fatigue ambience, hit, shield, elimination, zone, vote and result cues. Voice limits, cooldowns, master mute and caption equivalents are mandatory. Browser autoplay begins muted until explicit interaction.

## Accessibility

Meaning uses shape, text and value contrast in addition to colour. Captions reserve a non-overlapping region. Critical state is understandable with audio muted, reduced motion, reduced flash and compressed mobile viewing.

## Release-Governance Gates Implemented

### Freeze and Traceability

- Source, deterministic rules, snapshots, events, config, content, providers, assets, feature flags, owners, and rollback identity are canonical and deeply immutable.
- Material changes identify affected evidence and reset the relevant endurance or canary clocks.
- Every MUST requirement requires current exact-release evidence.
- Missing, stale, duplicate, wrong-source, wrong-release, malformed, blocked, or prohibited P0/P1-waiver evidence fails closed.

### Capacity and Endurance

- Capacity evaluation reports p50/p95/p99/max, budget headroom, and resource slopes.
- CI performance can prove software behavior but remains explicitly non-production-reference.
- Endurance requires real provenance and elapsed time; accelerated or synthetic timestamps cannot satisfy the 72-hour gate.

### Providers and Safety

- Provider evidence requires exact candidate binding, valid digest, current collection/expiry, credentialed operation, production-equivalent environment, external signature, and authentication/reconnect/duplicate/reversal/outage/rate checks.
- Security, privacy, moderation, accessibility, audiovisual, asset/licence, supply-chain, and platform-policy evidence requires current external production-equivalent review with no blocking finding.

### Operational Drills

All 26 mandatory scenarios pass deterministic implementation checks using real service, recovery, output-health, operator-control, and alert components. Production status remains blocked because CI drills are neither externally witnessed nor production-equivalent.

### Canary

- Thresholds are immutable before start.
- Samples must be exact-candidate bound, fresh, monotonic, digest-valid, externally signed, and collected in production.
- Integrity, privacy, control, moderation, restore, record, resource, output, and platform-policy triggers immediately require rollback.
- A material change resets the canary clock and samples.
- Synthetic or non-production runs cannot satisfy the seven-day gate.

### Final Assessor

The independent assessor returns:

- `FAIL` for integrity, safety, P0/P1, failed endurance, or rollback-trigger conditions;
- `BLOCKED` for incomplete or invalid external evidence;
- `PASS / R5` only when every exact-candidate real-world gate and the independent signed review succeeds.

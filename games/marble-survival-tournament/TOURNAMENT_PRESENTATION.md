# Tournament Presentation

## Structure

The recovered authority uses five elimination stages with quotas `[16, 8, 4, 2, 1]` for a standard 32-marble field. Existing round progression, qualification, elimination, championship and intermission semantics are preserved.

## Broadcast priorities

The redesigned shell targets gameplay dominance: the arena fills the stage, while standings and race-control events are compact translucent overlays. Clean feed hides header, tournament strip, leaderboard, event/influence panels and footer while keeping the arena and optional camera/feed label.

## Competitor recognition

Stable names/numbers remain in the DOM leaderboard. In-world marbles use stable colors plus geometric patterns and a physical identity fin, so recognition does not rely on labels or color alone.

## Elimination and finals

Elimination semantic events trigger a short danger burst and bounded camera impulse without stopping the round. Qualification gets a smaller success burst. Final/finish/victory camera directives tighten coverage without altering the solver. A confirmed champion receives a dedicated champion card and stronger short-lived visual treatment.

## Viewer integrity

The server continues to report viewer influence as unavailable until it can be routed through deterministic authority. Presentation does not fake votes or guaranteed outcomes.

## HUD-off standard

The intended acceptance question remains: with the HUD hidden, does the scene still read as marbles competing through a spatial survival course? That must be answered by live browser capture before final visual acceptance.

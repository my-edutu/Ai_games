# Art Direction

## Goal

Make Marble Survival readable as a physics tournament with the HUD hidden. Marbles, danger, route direction and eliminations must read from world presentation first.

## Visual language

- physically shaded marbles with clearcoat and strong contact shadows;
- restrained emissive cues reserved for rails, hazards and finals;
- large readable machinery silhouettes instead of decorative clutter;
- arena edges/support structures that sell depth and elevation;
- compact broadcast overlays rather than dashboard panels;
- no fake CSS perspective, no glowing circles on flat rectangles.

## Arena identities

- **Seeding Sprint — Neon Circuit:** cool technical track, cyan rail energy, clean speed-readability.
- **Gate Gauntlet — Industrial Factory:** steel, warm machinery accents, heavy mechanical forms.
- **Hazard Circuit — Ice Lab:** pale reflective architecture and cold hazard lighting.
- **Final Four — Sky Temple:** elevated stone/gold presentation, columns and larger void context.
- **Championship — Volcanic Forge:** dark forge materials, orange emissive rails and ember dressing.

These themes change world dressing, track profile, lighting and machinery presentation rather than only recoloring a single flat board.

## Competitor identity

Stable competitor palettes are reinforced by `ring`, `split`, `chevron` and `dots` geometry plus a small physical identity fin. Identity must remain recognizable without relying solely on color.

## Effects rule

Effects are event-causal. Contacts create small bounded bursts; elimination, shield recovery, qualification and championship events get stronger but short-lived treatment. No continuous particle fog over gameplay.

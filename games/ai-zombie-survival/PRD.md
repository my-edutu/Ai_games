# Product Requirements — AI Zombie Survival

## Product promise

An autonomous four-survivor squad prepares a refuge by day and survives escalating hordes by night while viewers track the current day, base integrity, survivor condition, resources, strategy and next crisis.

## Core requirements

- fixed-step replayable gameplay with automatic result, intermission and restart;
- constructive bounded worlds with valid gate-to-base and resource routes;
- role-specialized bounded survivor AI and deterministic horde navigation;
- causal combat, economy, construction, repair, healing, weather and evacuation progression;
- ten-second broadcast comprehension at mobile size, captions and accessible alternatives;
- safe fixed-choice audience influence with exactly-once application and complete no-audience mode;
- verified snapshots, writer fencing, recovery/quarantine, output health, bounded resources and operator runbooks;
- exact-candidate release validation that cannot synthesize provider, endurance, canary or independent-review evidence.

## Success measures

World validity is 100%; same-input replay divergence and duplicate authoritative audience application are zero; technical failures never count as losses; p99 authoritative tick stays inside the declared budget; browser captures preserve goal/progress/danger; no open P0/P1 finding remains at a phase exit.

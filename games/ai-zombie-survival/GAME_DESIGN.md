# Game Design — Last Light Protocol

**Primary progress:** Day.  
**Terminal success:** reach the configured evacuation day with at least one living survivor and an intact core.  
**Terminal loss:** all survivors die or the command core reaches zero.  
**Continuation:** causal result, records, short intermission and automatic seeded restart.

Preparation creates choices among scavenging, building, repair, treatment, rest and guard allocation. Horde phases create choices among interception, ranged defense, breach repair, rescue, retreat and last-stand protection. Difficulty expands through composition, gates, weather, scarcity, route pressure and concurrent objectives before raw stat inflation.

Target dramatic patterns are prepared dominance, early breach then repair, risky resource run, rescue under pressure, cascading collapse and last-stand evacuation. A bounded event director may select only authored eligible weather/encounter patterns; it cannot force a result.

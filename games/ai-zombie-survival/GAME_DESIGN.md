# Game Design

The player-facing story is generated from simulation truth. Survivors have roles and make local risk decisions. Scouts/scavengers seek loot, engineers repair damaged barricades, medics heal when medicine exists, and any survivor can fight or retreat based on threat and health. Zombies shift from wandering to pursuit and attacks, collide spatially, damage physical defenses and periodically reinforce the horde.

A 120-second day forms one broadcast-friendly day/night loop. The city contains residential, commercial, medical, civic, industrial and outskirts districts. Survival ends when all survivors die or the safe-house integrity collapses.

# Viewer Interaction

Viewers act as supporters, challengers and strategists through fixed choices. Approved effects are supply priority, fortification sector, scout route, spotlight, generator boost, medicine cache, ammunition cache, fog bank, runner surge and next-weather choice.

Provider payloads are authenticated and normalized outside the game. Requests use privacy-safe references, bounded enums, moderation, entitlement, rate, idempotency, game eligibility, event-director cooldown/conflict/cap, durable scheduling, acknowledgement, expiry and append-only reversal. No effect can directly set health, core integrity, result, record or unavoidable outcome. Autonomous play remains complete during all provider outages.

# Visual Acceptance Report

## Verified
- 2.5D isometric renderer compiles.
- Real-height buildings, district ground planes and depth sorting are implemented.
- Survivor/zombie articulated representations and action-driven animation mapping are implemented.
- Physical barricades and loot objects are rendered.
- Day/night/weather/VFX layers are implemented.
- Compact HUD and HUD-hidden control are implemented.
- Broadcast camera modes consume authoritative state.
- 16 automated tests pass, including 250-zombie sustained simulation.

## Not claimed
- Runtime screenshot evidence: managed Chromium reports organization policy blocking both `file://` and loopback URLs, so no truthful gameplay screenshot was produced in this environment.
- Multi-hour browser soak and OBS capture: not run.
- Full positional ambience/adaptive score: partial.
- Rescue/evacuation gameplay: not implemented.

The rebuild is therefore not marked production-complete despite the playable browser implementation being present.

# Survivor System

Roles: leader, scout, medic, defender, scavenger, engineer. Actions: idle, move, scavenge, aim, attack, reload, repair, retreat, heal, rescue, injured, dead. Health, stamina, infection, morale, ammunition, carried supply, threat, facing and cooldown are explicit authoritative fields.

Presentation maps low health to limping, combat to weapon-forward stance, work to bent interaction posture and retreat to sprint posture.

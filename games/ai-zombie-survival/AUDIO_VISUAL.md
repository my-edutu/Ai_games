# Audio / Visual Direction

Grounded ruined-city isometric 2.5D. Districts have restrained material palettes rather than neon-apocalypse styling. Buildings are extruded, shadows are projected, world entities are depth-sorted, survivors/zombies use articulated procedural silhouettes, and combat/scavenging/barricade events produce short-lived VFX.

Night uses darkening plus survivor-local light pools; rain/fog/storm are presentation effects. Audio currently uses lightweight generated event cues for shots, barricade impacts and critical survival moments so no licensed asset is required. Full positional ambience/music remains a production-polish item and is not claimed complete.

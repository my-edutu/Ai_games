# Audiovisual Direction

The refuge uses strong geometric silhouettes, daylight preparation warmth, dusk warning, cool high-contrast horde lighting, and clear danger/progress/value roles that survive grayscale and compression. The camera keeps survivors, the refuge and current breach pressure readable without exposing operator diagnostics.

Semantic audio states are preparation, dusk, horde, breach, recovery, evacuation, loss and safe maintenance. Critical cues always have captions or visual equivalents. Particle, camera, flash, voice and acknowledgement density are capped; reduced-motion, reduced-flash, high-contrast, muted and clean-feed modes preserve meaning.

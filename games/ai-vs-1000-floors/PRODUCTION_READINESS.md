# Production Readiness — AI vs 1,000 Floors

## Readiness ladder

- **R0:** specification only;
- **R1:** deterministic headless foundation;
- **R2:** production AI/content and statistical balance evidence;
- **R3:** representative broadcast experience and interaction safety;
- **R4:** software-complete operations/release candidate with synthetic/fixture evidence;
- **R5:** exact deployed production candidate with real provider, endurance, canary, drills and independent review.

## Current decision

Phase 1 is `PASS / R1`. It is not a launch candidate and must not be described as production-ready.

## Phase 1 demonstrated

- fixed-step serializable authority;
- named RNG and retained initial draws;
- constructive valid floors and deterministic repair;
- legal fallback actions and atomic rejection;
- complete 1,000-floor lifecycle and automatic restart;
- causal game results separate from technical restore errors;
- ordered event sequences and clean per-run window;
- checksummed snapshot and exact continuation;
- 16 tests, 128 generator seeds and three full evidence runs with zero integrity failure;
- authoritative nondeterminism scan clean.

## Required before R4

- ten sectors, enemies, hazards, modules, checkpoints, Wardens and Architect;
- production planner, stuck recovery and calibrated campaigns;
- responsive public stream, character/VFX/audio/accessibility evidence;
- safe provider-neutral influence and Chat vs AI;
- durable authority, leases, verified recovery, health probes, bounded resources and chaos;
- security/privacy review, runbooks, rollback, exact manifest and CI artifacts;
- zero open P0/P1 software finding.

## External blockers before R5

- current credentialed YouTube/Twitch/provider path validation;
- production-reference capacity and audiovisual capture validation;
- independent security, privacy, moderation and accessibility review;
- witnessed restore, credential revoke, provider disable and rollback drills;
- real uninterrupted 72-hour endurance on the frozen candidate;
- real seven-day guarded canary;
- independent exact-candidate production-readiness review.

Synthetic clocks, fixture providers, local browser tests and implementer review never satisfy these blockers.

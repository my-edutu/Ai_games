# Audiovisual direction

## World and cast

THOUSAND combines warm ivory stone, terracotta masonry, weathered copper, turquoise mechanisms, amber energy and botanical forms. The expedition takes place in a hollow sky citadel. Traversable platform tops must match the simulation; dressing must never imply a safe surface across a lethal-looking gap or obscure the next landing.

The original explorer and small hovering companion are the complete current cast. The explorer needs a readable face, layered torso/backpack, articulated shoulders/elbows and hips/knees, hands and boots. Motion should show anticipation, extension, landing compression and secondary equipment movement. No ASTRO BOT character or Nintendo/Sony asset is included.

Districts should vary architecture and props as well as light and palette: aqueduct arches and falling water; copper machinery; crystalline conservatory; coral archive; cloud garden; foundry; observatory; astral structures; crown ribs and celestial mechanisms. Actual capture review, rather than source complexity, determines whether these identities read.

## Camera, cues and sound

Follow framing must keep the explorer and next landing inside a safe screen region on desktop and portrait. Wide and orbit views serve scene inspection. Reduced motion suppresses decorative camera/secondary movement without changing campaign timing. Hazard visuals are driven by the snapshot's authoritative active state. Directional inlays, energy collectibles, a low exit beacon and contact shadows support route reading.

Audio is local synthesized Web Audio: an opt-in ambient layer and bounded event voices. It follows campaign event sequence numbers to avoid duplicate effects on resumed snapshots. It pauses with transport/visibility and disposes its resources. A working audio toggle is functional evidence, not a listening-quality verdict.

# Testing Strategy — AI vs 1,000 Floors

## Principle

Prove invariants and distributions using production rules, deterministic seeds and exact manifests. A showcase seed, average FPS or green unit suite alone cannot establish readiness.

## Layers

- configuration/contract tests;
- generator validity and deterministic repair corpora;
- action legality and atomic rejection;
- fallback/production AI legality and budget;
- ordered rule and terminal precedence tests;
- full 1,000-floor deterministic run;
- event/RNG/checksum replay equality;
- snapshot corruption/version/invariant restore tests;
- progression, sector, module and balance campaigns;
- render snapshot, browser layout, accessibility and capture tests;
- semantic audio priority/density/degradation tests;
- influence duplicate/reorder/expiry/reversal/provider-outage tests;
- lease/store/recovery/health/chaos tests;
- release manifest, evidence-class and truthful R-level tests.

## Phase 1 evidence corpus

- 16 Node tests;
- 128 generator seeds across the full floor range;
- deterministic repair mutation;
- complete safe 1,000-floor victory and automatic restart;
- parallel replay equality;
- snapshot restore/corruption/version rejection;
- floor timeout and stagnation classifications;
- three evidence runs totaling 24,938 logical ticks;
- zero invariant, replay and generator failure.

## Seed corpora

Smoke seeds, every discovered regression seed, sector/floor-feature strata, pathological topology, boss/checkpoint fixtures, max-progression, no-audience, typical/burst/adversarial influence, provider outage and restore/migration fixtures are retained rather than blacklisted.

## Failure classification

P0 integrity/security/data corruption; P1 requirement/recovery/gameplay blocker; P2 bounded quality defect; P3 refinement. Technical abort/quarantine is excluded from game win/loss distributions.

## Browser and audiovisual evidence

Phase 3 tests 1280×720 and 390×844, Canvas non-empty state, floor label, result/recovery truthfulness, reduced motion/flash, color-independent cues, safe-zone overflow, audio status and representative screenshots through the actual stream host.

## Operations evidence

Phase 5 injects worker kill, duplicate/reordered audience input, provider outage, persistence lag, corrupt snapshot, queue pressure, renderer/audio failure, frozen output and host restart; expected health state, public scene, action, alert and recovery are asserted.

# Product Requirements — AI vs 1,000 Floors

## Product outcome

Create an always-on autonomous livestream game whose progress, danger and intelligence are understandable within ten seconds and whose complete 1,000-floor run can be replayed exactly from version, configuration, seed and normalized event log.

## Audience

- passive livestream viewers who enjoy long record pursuits;
- strategy viewers who want to understand why an autonomous character acts;
- returning viewers who follow sectors, Wardens, records and evolving loadouts;
- supporters and challengers who use bounded fixed-choice influence;
- operators responsible for unattended stream health and truthful recovery.

## Core jobs

1. Enter the stream and immediately know Astra is climbing toward Floor 1,000.
2. See current progress, danger and AI intent without reading a diagnostic dashboard.
3. Observe meaningful tactical decisions, setbacks, adaptation and causal wins/losses.
4. Influence eligible future choices without buying a guaranteed result.
5. Trust records, replays and recovery because gameplay truth is deterministic and audited.

## Functional requirements

- exactly 1,000 authoritative floors organized into ten 100-floor sectors;
- deterministic constructive generation with validation and repair;
- autonomous play with legal-action filtering, bounded planning and fallback;
- health, energy, shield, credits and three module slots;
- checkpoints/minibosses at sector offsets 25, 50 and 75;
- Wardens every 100 floors and the Architect on Floor 1,000;
- result, decisive cause, intermission and automatic restart;
- accessible 16:9 browser source and mobile-safe composition;
- adaptive semantic audio with visual alternatives;
- fixed-choice votes/effects with idempotency, caps, expiry and audit;
- provider, renderer, audio, persistence and telemetry degradation;
- verified snapshot restore, quarantine and finite supervisor actions;
- headless campaigns, browser tests, chaos tests and release assessment.

## Non-functional requirements

- no ambient authoritative randomness or wall-clock outcome dependence;
- no remote model required for continuity;
- no raw provider payload, payment data, viewer text or secret in game state/public output;
- no unbounded planner, queue, event window, effect pool, audio voice or presentation resource;
- technical failure is never counted as gameplay loss;
- floor generation, AI, simulation, presentation and recovery meet declared p99 budgets;
- critical information remains distinguishable without color, motion or audio;
- no R5 claim without external exact-candidate evidence.

## Success measures

- ten-second comprehension pass rate;
- floor progress, run duration and terminal-reason distributions;
- invalid-floor, illegal-action, fallback, stuck and replay-divergence rates;
- three or more healthy dramatic-pattern distributions;
- module, sector, enemy and tactical-path diversity;
- meaningful-event and vote-consequence cadence;
- frame/tick/audio/output p99 and bounded memory/resource slopes;
- provider duplicate/reconnect/reversal correctness;
- recovery success, quarantine correctness and rollback drills.

## Phase 1 acceptance

Phase 1 passes when deterministic config/state, constructive floors, action legality, fallback AI, ordered rules, 1,000-floor completion, causal terminal results, auto-restart, event sequencing, checksummed snapshots, exact restore, seed corpora and headless evidence pass with no open P0/P1.

## Risks and controls

- **Repetition:** sector mechanics, topology features, module choices and event-pattern metrics rather than palette swaps.
- **Omniscient AI:** observations and planner knowledge remain bounded; public intent is templated rather than hidden reasoning.
- **Pay-to-win:** entitlement buys only disclosed bounded eligibility/weight.
- **Long-run corruption:** fenced authority, append-only evidence, checksums, invariant validation and quarantine.
- **Visual overload:** progress-first hierarchy, semantic priority and density caps.
- **False readiness:** software completion stops at R4 until real soak/canary/provider/independent evidence exists.

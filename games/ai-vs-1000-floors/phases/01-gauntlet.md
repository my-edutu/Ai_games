## Acceptance sequence

1. Establish an executable finite 1,000-floor core and one authoritative browser worker.
2. Make the complete current cast and ten districts inspectable in a real 3D scene.
3. Verify seeded completion, hazard truth, save continuation and checkpoint recovery.
4. Exercise real desktop/portrait controls, storage failures and resource lifecycle.
5. Compare actual moving output directly to the fixed reference, identify the largest remaining gap and send it to the responsible builder.
6. Repeat independent review and improvement without a preset round limit; retain each meaningful iteration on the live progress page.
7. Publish the reviewable branch and draft pull request with exact test results and remaining gaps. A draft is not a benchmark win or production approval.

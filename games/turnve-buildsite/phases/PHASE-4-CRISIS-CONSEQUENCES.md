# Phase 4 — Crisis & Consequences

## Goal

Apply realistic cost/schedule/safety/quality pressure and evaluate action sequence and authority boundaries.

## Implemented

- Early truck, waiting exposure, approaching/active rain, missing sign-off, foreman pressure.
- Temporary hold recommendation.
- Expedited consultant inspection.
- Supplier update and QS cost inquiry.
- Weather material protection.
- Deliberately unsafe “allow pour yourself” path with authority and quality penalties.
- Safe handoff back to authorized site leadership without granting intern approval authority.
- Decision/consequence audit timeline.

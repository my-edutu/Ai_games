# Turnve BuildSite — Simulation Design

## Core loop

Task → explore → inspect → collect evidence → interpret → communicate → act → document → consequence.

## Player verbs

Walk, look, inspect, photograph, classify, note, compare, contact, ask, recommend, escalate, wait, protect, clear, draft, submit.

## Pressure design

The crisis deliberately combines an early concrete truck, potential waiting charges, approaching rain, unsigned inspection, and foreman schedule pressure. Safety-first behavior without communication is safer but inefficient; communication-only behavior without issue resolution is incomplete; unauthorized progress creates severe downstream quality and trust penalties.

## Guided mode

Objective markers, interaction highlights, TARI hints, control coaching, and recovery actions are visible.

## Assessment mode

Markers are reduced, hints limited, timing stricter, and scoring remains independent.

## Completion

The scenario ends only after the crisis response and four required artifacts are submitted or the presenter jumps to a report in demo mode. The report preserves incomplete/missed-work penalties.

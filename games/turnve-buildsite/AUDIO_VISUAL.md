# Turnve BuildSite — Audio & Visual Direction

## Visual language

Stylized professional low-poly construction technology. Theme tokens: deep navy, construction yellow, safety orange, white, slate, success green, alert red.

## Environment

Gate/security, site office, safety board, storage, rebar/formwork, partially completed structure, temporary access, open edge, slab/pour zone, truck waiting area, crane zone, rest area. Props use original procedural geometry and simple materials.

## Motion

Opening fly-through, crane rotation, worker waypoint/idle motion, truck arrival, moving clouds/rain, hazard correction state changes, subtle UI transitions.

## Audio

Optional construction ambience, footsteps, radio notifications, UI confirmation/warning, truck arrival, rain, office ambience, report transition. Captions mirror important radio cues. No primary action depends on audio.

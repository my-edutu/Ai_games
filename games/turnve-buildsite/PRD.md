# Turnve BuildSite — Product Requirements

## Objective

Demonstrate Turnve’s experiential-learning value through a polished construction internship simulation that can be understood in a live pitch without continuous narration.

## Learner outcome

A student or recent graduate practices intern-level site coordination: observation, evidence capture, drawing control, inspection readiness, stakeholder communication, escalation, documentation, and trade-off management.

## Critical user story

As a construction intern, I must help prepare a slab for a safe, approved concrete pour while schedule/cost pressure increases, so I can demonstrate that I know how to protect safety and quality without exceeding my authority.

## Required flow

Cinematic → PPE → briefing → site walk → drawing comparison → pre-pour checklist → pressure event → consequences → four artifacts → readiness report.

## Required issues

Fall protection, blocked emergency route, wet cement storage, water/electrical proximity, incomplete formwork, outdated drawing, missing consultant sign-off, early truck, rain.

## Success criteria

- Explorable 3D site and functional interaction loop.
- Minimum five discoverable issues.
- Evidence capture and stakeholder communication alter state.
- Drawing Revision 02/03 discrepancy is discoverable.
- Pre-pour checklist rejects false confidence through assessment penalties.
- Truck/rain crisis is reliable in demo mode.
- Decisions change safety/quality/schedule/cost/communication and trust.
- Four artifacts are editable and influence evaluation.
- Final report explains evidence and consequence chains.
- Guided and Pitch Demo modes reset reliably.

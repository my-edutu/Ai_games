# Testing Strategy

Phase tests progress from deterministic authority through adaptive AI, broadcast, interaction, recovery, and release governance. Tests use seeded reruns, invariant checks, corruption injection, provider outages, queue pressure, renderer failure, deterministic campaigns, accelerated soak, chaos scenarios, source/privacy scans, stream self-tests, and Playwright desktop/mobile captures.

CI builds from the locked toolchain, runs the complete repository suite, scans authoritative traffic directories for ambient nondeterminism, produces Phase 5 and Phase 6 JSON evidence, installs Chromium, verifies all stream sources, and uploads traffic capture and validation artifacts.

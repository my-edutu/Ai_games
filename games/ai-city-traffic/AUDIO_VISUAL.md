# Audiovisual Direction

The city is presented as a dark premium systems map with visible lane classes, heat, closures, signal-axis glyphs, and shape-coded cars, buses, and emergency vehicles. The permanent HUD prioritizes Mobility Score, flow, congestion, trips, active vehicles, wave, record gap, and AI intent.

Semantic audio maps signal changes, incidents, recoveries, trip bursts, waves, audience effects, and results to bounded synthesized cues. Captions provide the same critical information. Reduced-motion and reduced-flash users receive a complete experience without animation dependence. Audio failure cannot stop the simulation.

# Product requirements

## Viewer promise

Watch a coastal district keep moving as autonomous people and vehicles negotiate changing demand. A viewer should identify the objective (keep trips completing), current pressure (queues), and the effect of an intervention within ten seconds.

## Requirements and ownership

| Requirement | Observable acceptance | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Autonomous continuity | Completed trips continue across the declared long-run seed corpus; active and pending populations stay bounded. | Simulation |
| Believable traffic | Vehicles follow connected lanes, keep spacing and receive junction permission before entering. | Simulation |
| Citizens | Pedestrians have destinations, visible walking gait and protected crossings; people do not occupy the same corner. | Simulation / world |
| Honest interaction | Demand, policy and roadworks buttons change authoritative snapshot fields and subsequent behavior. | Simulation / UI |
| Finished scene | Actual aerial and street captures show modeled vehicles/citizens, varied buildings, roads, markings, greenery and waterfront. | World |
| Understandable UI | Real metrics and controls remain visible at 390×844; keyboard and reduced-motion modes work. | UI |
| Review evidence | Each meaningful iteration has dated output, reviewer findings and next gaps in the live journal. | Lead |

## Scope

A locally controllable autonomous simulation is included. Hosted audience accounts, payments, external AI services, city construction, multiplayer and production operations are not implemented by this build. No paid effect or outcome is implied. These limits do not lower the visual benchmark.

## Quality bar

Representative actual Cities: Skylines II gameplay, compared at matching camera scale, viewport and traffic density. Transport Fever 2 supports vehicle/infrastructure detail; Mini Motorways supports interface clarity. Capture provenance and actual media inspection are required before a verdict.

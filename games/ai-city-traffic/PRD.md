# Product Requirements — AI City Traffic Experiment

## Objective

Create a premium autonomous traffic experiment that can run continuously, explain its own strategy, generate readable crises and recoveries, support bounded audience influence, and remain operationally safe for livestream use.

## Primary viewer jobs

- Understand within seconds whether the city is flowing or approaching gridlock.
- Watch an autonomous intelligence diagnose pressure and change policy.
- Anticipate demand waves, incidents, recoveries, records, and run results.
- Influence eligible policies without purchasing a guaranteed outcome.

## Functional requirements

1. Deterministic fixed-tick city authority with connected road graphs, legal signals, collision-free lane occupancy, typed outcomes, automatic intermission, and restart.
2. Adaptive signal control, congestion-aware routing, bounded replanning, demand waves, incidents, transit and emergency priority, and public intent.
3. OBS-ready responsive canvas presentation, phone-readable HUD, captions, reduced motion/flash, semantic audio, replay, health, and safe recovery scene.
4. Authenticated, moderated, idempotent fixed-choice audience effects with equal safeguards for free and paid-eligible inputs.
5. Verified candidate-compatible snapshots, deterministic restore, invariant quarantine, health actions, resource caps, chaos drills, and rollback.
6. Candidate-bound release manifest, six-phase traceability, final campaigns, CI evidence, readiness score, and fail-closed R5 assessment.

## Nonfunctional requirements

- No ambient randomness or wall clock in authoritative rules, runtime, AI, generation, or state.
- Maximum route expansion: 512 nodes per decision.
- Maximum vehicles: 1,500; stream reference: 620.
- Audience queue: 64; retained semantic events: 8,192; presentation replay: 360 frames.
- Public state contains no seeds, run IDs, viewer tokens, raw payloads, rate windows, or authority-only demand state.
- Provider and moderation outage must disable interaction without stopping autonomous play.

## Success measures

Zero collision/invariant failures, deterministic reruns, zero duplicate applications, no prohibited terminal effects, multiple causal dramatic patterns, bounded resources, recoverable output, privacy-safe public state, and truthful release status.

# Production Readiness

The codebase satisfies the six software phases and produces a deterministic R4 release candidate. Software promotion fails on collisions, invariant or technical outcomes, replay divergence, duplicate effects, unauthorized controls, private exposure, open P0/P1, nondeterministic rerun, prohibited terminal influence, chaos failure, traceability gaps, capacity failure, or missing drill implementation.

R5 promotion is separately blocked unless the exact deployed candidate has production-reference capacity, real 72-hour endurance, current credentialed provider evidence, external safety attestations, independently witnessed production drills, a real seven-day canary, and independent exact-candidate review. Synthetic or CI self-attested evidence cannot satisfy these gates.

# Game Design

A run begins with a generated road network and calm demand. Demand moves through rush, surge, incident, and recovery waves. Vehicles seek distinct boundary destinations, queue at signals, reroute around closures, and complete trips. The AI protects citywide mobility through legal adaptive phases and bounded corridor policies.

The primary score rewards completed trips, bus and emergency throughput, and sustained operation while penalizing dropped demand and average delay. Sustained near-total waiting produces gridlock; reaching the configured experiment duration produces cycle completion. Both outcomes show a result, record comparison, short intermission, and automatic new city.

Drama comes from pressure and recovery: rush-hour mastery, incident cascades, transit trade-offs, near-gridlock escapes, and legitimate overload loss. No director function writes a win or loss directly.

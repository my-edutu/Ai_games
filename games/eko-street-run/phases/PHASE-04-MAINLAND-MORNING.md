# Phase 04 — Mainland Morning Vertical Slice

## Status

`IN PROGRESS — RED vertical-slice corpus authored before implementation`

## Purpose

Turn the verified movement and character contracts into the first recognizable Lagos-inspired Eko Run world while keeping gameplay truth deterministic and presentation-only systems downstream of immutable snapshots/events.

## Acceptance criteria

- Mainland Morning reads as Lagos through combined road, drainage, transport, commerce, architecture, pedestrian-motion and soundscape cues rather than labels/flags/stereotypes.
- Real Three.js scene construction exists but cannot enter or mutate authoritative state.
- Player, safe route, upcoming decision space and progress are visible within five seconds on desktop and mobile-safe framing.
- Camera look-ahead reveals required route information before commitment and has no camera-caused failure in the representative Phase 4 corpus.
- Quality degradation removes ambience/distant detail before player, route, decision/cue or progress information.
- Muted mode preserves caption/visual alternatives; reduced motion removes camera shake and reduces non-critical motion without hiding state.
- Same public snapshot/options reconstruct the same presentation model independent of 30/60/120 presentation sampling.
- All four Phase 3 outfit families remain readable against Mainland Morning presentation constraints.
- Presentation generation meets p99 <4 ms and worst <16.67 ms in CI evidence.
- Phase 1–3 regressions stay green.
- Three adversarial 22-skill review/improvement passes have no unresolved Phase 4 stop-ship/P1/P2 finding.

## Phase boundary

Danfo, drains, potholes, crowds and roadworks may be previewed visually in Phase 4 but do not gain authoritative damage/collision consequences until Phase 5 fairness contracts and deterministic hazard tests own them.

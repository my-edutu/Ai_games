# Game Design — Endless Adventure

## Dramatic loop
Explore a labyrinth → intercept binding guards and acquire relics → break the Warden's first-phase seal → survive its telegraphed phases → clear remaining hostiles → reach the portal → descend. The seal is mechanically real: while living defenders sustain it, early Warden hits are weakened; it never makes a route impassable. The guardian's summoned reinforcements remain real threats. Each seal break has a bounded authoritative event and corresponding scene/audio feedback. The 3D viewer never owns damage or progression. Lose all heroes → play a short legible intermission → start a new expedition. The camera follows the active party.

## Roles
- **Ashen Vanguard:** front-line melee and passage-breaking.
- **Wildshadow:** mobile ranged coverage, high single-target attack range.
- **Starweaver:** arcane attacks and periodic targeted healing.
- **Revenant:** ordinary close-range pressure.
- **Cultist:** fragile cult fighter that joins ambushes.
- **Warden:** designated boss protecting the portal with high health.

## Content
One connected 19×19 maze per floor; spawn fixed at (1,1), exit is the farthest reachable tile. Each floor contains a boss, bounded ordinary enemies and healing relics. Themes cycle among four visual palettes/names, with encounter difficulty increasing over depth. End-state is determined only by rules, not a script.

## Visual identity
Dark slate masonry, gold and sea-glass magic, high-contrast silhouettes, readable teal hero versus ember enemy effects. Isometric-following camera retains spatial understanding. HUD shows depth, objective, team survival and recent events. No paid or hidden outcome manipulation.

## Next quality gates
Telegraphed boss phases, hit-stop confined to presentation, authored 3D character skins, richer animation blending, dynamic encounter selection, branching room grammar, environmental set pieces and audio with captions. Review action readability before VFX density.

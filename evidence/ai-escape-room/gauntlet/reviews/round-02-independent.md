## Method and limits

Unblinded, independent inspection of the nine images listed below. No implementation or builder reports read; no browser used. ## Single biggest remaining gap

**Authored shape and material richness at the focal mechanism.** In `mechanism.png`, smooth rings/rods, a flat black center, broad trim and coarse wear dominate. The reference safe/workbench has layered housings, mechanically connected components, toothed profiles, fasteners and fine material variation. The gap is visible at comparable closeup purpose.

**Concrete next target:** finish the final vault as a hero assembly: layered recessed plates/housings; visibly connected gears, latches and pivots; beveled profiles; engraved dial marks; purposeful fasteners; distinct brass and iron surface response. Keep the mechanism readable. Capture the same closeup and room view: the additional construction and material separation must remain evident at both scales. Adding more generic props alone will not resolve this gap.
